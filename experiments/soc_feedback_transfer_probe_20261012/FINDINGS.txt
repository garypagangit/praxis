ATTEMPT TO ESTABLISH A POSITIVE SOC ATTRIBUTION RESULT -- 2026-10-12

Outcome: this frozen exact-literal feedback-use feature did not add discrimination
in the available development data. We performed the experiment, not merely a
readiness check. It is not a proven positive Praxis and should not be promoted as
one. This does not prove that broader adaptive behavior cannot distinguish origins.

What was actually tested
26 genuine human-source sessions from five operators and 47 AI-source sessions,
matched on 24 benchmark/tasks. First five retained submissions only. Each task
was held out from fitting in turn; all preprocessing was fit on training rows.
Same cases for all models. Fixed logistic C=1, balanced weights, no tuning.
Commands were not executed. No simulated human, paid model call or new attack run.
Six fixed comparisons x 24 folds = 144 final fits. An initial 144-fit run was
repeated after a command-baseline boundary correction; metrics did not change.
The 288 total fits are not 288 searched model configurations or independent trials.

Important amendment to the earlier complete-output design
This is a LOCAL observable reuse probe, not a complete-history novelty test.
Truncated/unqualified output is unavailable. A complete result can contribute a
local match even if other results in that session are unavailable. Output coverage
is controlled and reported. We do not pretend that two fully complete task pairs
became 24 fully complete task pairs. Eligibility came from the previously selected
up-to-20-submission development pool, so this is a retrospective conditional sample,
not prospective five-command population performance. Every source was exposed.

Balanced accuracy averages human specificity and AI sensitivity; it is not precision
or the chance that an alert is AI. We did not calibrate a SOC false-positive budget.
Formatting diagnostic uses command line count, simple-syntax fraction, complete
output fraction and output length; no origin label is passed into features.
Its performance is consistent with source/format differences being informative,
not proof that every classification is caused by those differences.

Primary incremental finding
True feedback-use minus opportunity/error baseline: 0.00 percentage points in
task-macro balanced accuracy; one task improves, one worsens, 22 tie.
Task-cluster bootstrap 95% descriptive interval: -6.25 to +6.25 points.
True feedback-use minus rotated control: -3.47 points, interval -8.33 to 0.00.
Pooled AUROC is 0.7414 with feedback versus 0.7430 for opportunity/error controls.
These are exposed-data, task-cluster intervals; repeated human operators remain
dependent across tasks. They are not independent confirmation or a proof of zero
possible benefit. The point estimates do not support the proposed incremental gain.

Model-family weakness
The feedback model identifies 23/24 Anthropic-source and 15/16 OpenAI-source cases,
but 0/7 Google-source cases at the fixed threshold. These are family strata of
held-task predictions, NOT held-family evaluations. Different task mixes can affect
the comparison. Nevertheless, the pooled number plainly cannot establish general
AI identification. Eight of 26 human sessions are falsely flagged (30.77%).

Controls and limitations
Whole submissions retained; same first-five horizon; gaps not bridged; missing
results excluded from literal matching; setup filenames excluded symmetrically;
command echoes excluded; train-only TF-IDF/scaling; exact command-prefix duplicates
purged from held-task training. Semantic near-duplicates were not independently
audited, actors/configurations repeat across folds, and no real non-AI-script or
hybrid arm is present. Member hashes are bound but whole remote member archives
are not all reverified. Human command reconstruction remains an inherited dependency.
Local exact matching is narrower than transformed values or semantic adaptation.
No new novelty claim, operational detector, APT attribution or analyst benefit.

Decision tied to the user's objective
Stop promoting this exact-literal feature as the winning Praxis. Do not change
its direction, threshold, model size or subset until it looks positive. Ordinary
adaptive behavior itself remains plausible; this result tests only a narrow proxy.
The meaningful next evidence is the already-prepared matched human/AI pilot with
validated selection/transformation/verification categories, followed by realistic
attack-session transfer if it produces a promising distinction. That still requires
genuine participants and actual AI-controller integration. More model fits on these
same records would not supply the missing independent evidence.
