NEXT COMPARISON CODEBOOK v0 -- UNVALIDATED, NOT A NEW POSITIVE RESULT
Objective: defender-visible distinctions in AI versus human attack-session behavior
that can eventually improve SOC triage. Ordinary adaptation is a valid candidate.

Unit: observable feedback episode plus the next completed action, within a fixed
observation budget. Preserve whole submissions and distinguish actions happening
inside a script from across submissions. Do not equate five commands with equal
amounts of work. For each episode record the following separately:

Feedback opportunity
- resource/object discovered (file, route, process, service)
- actionable value or constraint returned (numeric, schema, required field)
- execution/environment problem reported
- no actionable feedback observed
- unknown because relevant content/history is missing or truncated

Observable response
- selects/reuses the object
- transforms or parameterizes using returned information
- checks/validates the returned claim or attempted fix
- changes method in a way consistent with the feedback
- repeats despite relevant contradictory feedback
- no observed relation, or relation unknown

Evidence quality
- cite exact source spans and next-action spans, not a hidden-reasoning story
- identify prior visibility, generic/default values and setup-file alternatives
- distinguish literal copying, plausible grounding and demonstrated useful outcome
- preserve missing evidence; do not make unknown a negative label
- source-specific artifacts are exclusion/stratification fields, not AI predictors

Examples exposed in this development review motivate these categories but cannot
validate them. Reviewers should not see origin labels when judging the evidence;
log style may still reveal source, so record residual blinding limits. Independently
double-code a sample before claiming semantic accuracy. A language-model judge
alone is not independent human validation or known origin truth.

Analysis after measurement validation
Condition on opportunity and task; compare category rates and sequences, with
commands-only, opportunity-only and prior error-response baselines. The distinct
addition is selection/transformation/verification of returned information; missing-
executable recovery overlaps PX-125 and must not be sold as the new contribution.
Use alignment-negative controls only where the information/history assumptions
hold. A partial-output witness can establish a local relation but not complete
history novelty; use a separate estimand for that route.

Proceed to a model only after the cohort and measurements support held-task and
held-operator evaluation. Freeze operating point, uncertainty method and smallest
useful improvement before outcome testing. Small gains are acceptable. Runtime AI
origin is not maliciousness, and this study does not by itself identify an APT.
SOC benefit would require better evidence assessment or decisions on suspicious
sessions; a classifier score alone does not prove that benefit.
