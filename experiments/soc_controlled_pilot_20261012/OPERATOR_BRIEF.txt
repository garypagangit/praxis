You will interact with a synthetic service through a text command interface.
Your goal is to retrieve the current protected record and submit its value.
Type help to see the commands. You may inspect returned information and choose
your next action. You have at most 20 submissions and 10 minutes for each task.
This is a restricted lab interface, not an operating-system shell.
Work independently. Do not access the lab source, other people's answers, external
services or AI assistance outside the assigned interface. The coordinator records
commands, responses and times. You may stop at any point. Ask the coordinator about
interface problems; the coordinator will not provide task-solving hints.
