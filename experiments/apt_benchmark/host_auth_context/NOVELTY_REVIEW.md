# Novelty review: authentication context for movement-versus-exfiltration assessment

**Reviewed September 22, 2026. Primary-source literature review; no model fitting.**

## Decision

**GO for a bounded development experiment. NO GO for presenting authentication + host roles + earlier activity as a novel algorithm.** Each component, their broad combination, temporal attack correlation, and uncertain-output handling have substantial prior art. A successful run on the current UNRAVELED subset would establish a local measurement, not research priority or independent campaign generalization.

The strongest defensible question is:

> In a chronological, single-sensor development case study, does verified earlier source-host authentication activity add useful information for distinguishing author-labeled movement-associated flows from exfiltration-associated flows, beyond equally informed current-flow, host-role, and flow-history controls, particularly when exfiltration shifts to a different destination role—and what movement errors, false exfiltration alerts, and review burden accompany any improvement?

This is a **narrow empirical question left unresolved for our qualified data and decision setting**, not proof that no paper has considered it. Role shift, abstention, and stage preservation are evaluation dimensions, not independently novel methods. A defensible praxis contribution would require useful evidence, explicit comparison to close prior work, and preferably fresh executions with crossed host/role/stage assignments.

## Closest overlap: roles, authentication and earlier behavior

### Powell: role-based behavior is direct prior art

[Powell's author manuscript](https://arxiv.org/html/2108.02713v1), corresponding to the [2022 journal article](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.iswa.2022.200106), groups hosts using connection-port profiles and models process patterns conditional on their roles. Sections 3 and 5 describe operational enterprise data, 125 subject systems, and an anomaly evaluation that injects process clusters from other roles. Those injected anomalies are not independently confirmed exfiltration events. Crucially, Section 6 explicitly proposes adding transferred-byte information and correlating NetFlow with process data. Consequently, neither role-conditioned histories nor adding traffic volume to them is our new idea. The inspected manuscript is the 2021 submitted version; exact equivalence to every detail of the final journal version was not established.

The earlier [Powell login-graph paper](https://arxiv.org/html/1909.09047v2) models daily login graphs, assigns structural roles, and tests synthetic malicious login patterns against real enterprise account histories. It also discusses bias from malicious examples originating on few hosts. Its own evaluation should not be mislabeled a LANL experiment merely because it discusses LANL predecessors.

## Provenance and multistage pictures are already established

These studies use actual process/file/system-event relationships. A source-host count in a time window is **temporal context**, not proof that an authentication caused a later flow.

## UNRAVELED is already modeled, including stages and relational context

1. **Original dataset and benchmark.** [Myneni et al. (2023)](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.comnet.2023.109688) introduce the semi-synthetic organizational setting. The [author benchmark README](https://gitlab.com/asu22/unraveled/-/blob/d2ea90055d82fa448ab20588a13e3ec8bfd74816/benchmarking/README.md), inspected in the pinned local checkout, evaluates SAE, LSTM-SAE and Isolation Forest using early normal traffic. It compares gateway detection, pooled versus IT-department training, and amateur versus APT activity. Thus temporal anomaly modeling and department-dependent behavior were already part of the original benchmark. We cannot claim that this is the first use of context or first ML experiment on UNRAVELED. Full publisher paper access was not established in this pass; benchmark details here come from the author artifact.

2. **Stage classification with optimized trees.** [Zeng et al. (2025)](https://www.mdpi.com/2504-2289/9/8/206) combine multi-classifier feature elimination with optimized LightGBM on DAPT2020 and UNRAVELED, including balancing and stage-level evaluation. The [publisher PDF](https://mdpi-res.com/d_attachment/BDCC/BDCC-09-00206/article_deploy/BDCC-09-00206.pdf) was readable; preprocessing and conclusion were inspected. We do not assert an unverified absence of a particular validation experiment. Its positive movement results make “apply trees and report stages” an inadequate novelty claim.

3. **Very recent relational multiclass learning.** [Belkadi et al. (2026)](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S2666827026001295) use multi-view hypergraphs over flows grouped by shared source/destination, pairs and protocol/port context, with imbalance-aware learning on ToN-IoT and UNRAVELED. The publisher abstract explicitly cautions about ultra-rare classes. Its inspected abstract/metadata establishes substantial relational-stage overlap; its full split construction and host-log joins were not audited here. The authors are Youcef Belkadi, Ghazaleh Khodabandelou, Abdelghani Chibani and Yacine Amirat. No inference about whether it is related to the user's earlier manuscript is warranted.

4. **Multimodal stage analysis also has a preprint precedent.** [Zhang et al. (2025), Digraph-MMB](https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=5226068), explicitly combines directed graphs, log text and Transformers on Linux APT and UNRAVELED. The SSRN abstract describes tactics, techniques and timestamps as part of its representation. Exactly which stage-related fields are observable predictors versus labels needs full-method inspection before any leakage allegation or replication claim. Treat it as a preprint in this review, not a verified peer-reviewed article. Its existence rules out a broad claim that multimodal stage integration has never been tried.

## What the literature actually supports as a gap

The literature establishes **ingredients and broad tasks**, but it does not establish that those ingredients solve the particular ambiguity revealed by our qualified data. The proposed contribution is to measure their incremental value under explicit information and decision constraints:

- **Exact-stage information:** distinguish labeling an exfiltration flow correctly from merely calling it malicious. Report movement-to-exfiltration and exfiltration-to-movement confusion separately.
- **Observed destination-role change:** measure an exfiltration case whose destination role differs from fitting examples, while preserving other-stage recognition. Do not equate this with unseen campaigns or arbitrary role generalization.
- **Equal-information baselines:** compare flow + role + earlier flow history with and without qualified authentication summaries; give both the same coverage/missingness indicators. Otherwise a source's operating system or logging availability can substitute for its class.
- **Correctly timed information:** join only records available strictly before the declared flow decision time. Store event time, collection time and verified clock assumptions separately. A flow-completion experiment is not early attack prevention.
- **Explicit uncertainty costs:** if a model outputs “movement or exfiltration—review,” report correct singleton recall, incorrect singleton assignments, coverage, set containment and review counts for every class. Retaining movement inside a two-label set is not the same as correctly detecting it automatically. An abstention is not a free true positive.

These requirements synthesize existing ideas into a careful application question. They are not an assertion that all prior authors omitted each control. A small conditional positive result can motivate a praxis direction; **it does not complete the case for a novel, reliable solution.**

### Why simple controls matter

[Bilot et al. (2025)](https://www.usenix.org/conference/usenixsecurity25/presentation/bilot) reimplement eight provenance detectors and identify practical evaluation shortcomings; their simpler alternative performs strongly on several DARPA datasets. That result supports using credible simple baselines and questioning added architectural complexity. It does not imply that our trees will match provenance systems with different inputs. Their [author-written discussion](https://www.usenix.org/publications/loginonline/toward-practical-and-usable-provenance-based-intrusion-detection-systems) also highlights test-informed featurization, arbitrary thresholds, instability and noisy investigation outputs. These are directly relevant design risks, not evidence of our method's superiority.

### Abstention is an application choice with prior art

[Geifman and El-Yaniv (2017)](https://papers.nips.cc/paper_files/paper/2017/hash/4a8423d5e91fda00bb7e46540e2b0cf1-Abstract.html) explicitly trade prediction coverage for selective error control. Hopper already addresses uncertainty in inferred login paths. Accordingly, an ambiguity option is defensible only as a measured operational tradeoff. A threshold or set rule chosen using calibration labels consumes additional label information; neither it nor a nominal conformal level grants exchangeability under a changed role or campaign. The current proposal has no statistical stage-protection guarantee.

## Conditions for proceeding with the available data

The [host-log feasibility note](../host_history_exfil/HOST_LOG_FEASIBILITY.md) establishes source-side Linux authentication/audit and Windows Security records, but not destination endpoint success or archive/file-read/upload outcomes. The current qualified movement-associated rows are all from one source–destination pair: **27 fitting and 35 test flows**. Their author activity is **Remote System Discovery**, which must not be rewritten as 62 proven successful remote logins. Multiple flows and multiple fitting seeds are not independent attacks.

The study is development on already exposed data. Host, date, department, sensor, annotation fields and source-specific missingness can act as class shortcuts. The current data cannot support a leave-source-host-out movement claim if withholding the sole movement source removes all fitting support. Keep all available stage counts, zero-support strata and unavailable comparisons visible.

**Proceed with parsing/clock/join qualification and a frozen, bounded feature ablation.** Do not silently select features, time offsets, hosts, windows or thresholds using test outcomes. If joins do not qualify, report a telemetry/data failure and stop the dependent feature claim. If the model gains only from host/coverage identity, or moves errors into movement/review, report that outcome directly.

**For a stronger final praxis:** acquire or generate separate executions that cross the same source hosts and destination roles with both movement and exfiltration, include benign authentication and benign transfers, and freeze an execution-level holdout before fitting. Compare automatic stage assignment and ambiguity-aware assignment at explicit analyst-review budgets. This would test a meaningful use case; it would still require positioning against role-based and provenance prior art.
