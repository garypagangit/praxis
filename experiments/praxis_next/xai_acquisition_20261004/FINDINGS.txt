PX-107A: Explanation-guided evidence acquisition - development feasibility
October 4, 2026

DECISION
The AIT setup cannot test recovery of previously missed exfiltration episodes.
Stop promotion to a larger run. This is a data/task qualification outcome, not
evidence that explanation-guided acquisition is ineffective on suitable data.

WHAT WAS ACTUALLY DONE
Prepared frozen inputs for the three existing LightGBM seeds, forward-fold
explanation generation, five matched learned selectors and three simple ranking
baselines. Verified a local TreeSHAP probe against saved predictions. Committed
the pilot protocol and runtime before launch. Started one watchdog-protected AWS
worker. The worker completed the first seed's five selector fits and Wilson
evaluation, then was cancelled before a complete comparison was produced.
Partial selector output is not presented as a completed efficacy result.

The decisive ceiling audit was completed after launch. It should have preceded
training. It independently reconstructs source/destination/5-minute cases and
checks frozen input hashes, episode coverage and acquisition headroom. All 47
checks pass on 1,067,211 distinct evaluation rows, reused across three seeds.

Each execution has one exfiltration episode proxy under the frozen definition,
and every baseline seed warns within it. Every five-minute case containing an
exfiltration-labeled flow also already has a warning. The missed flows therefore
belong to already-warned cases. Investigating only unwarned cases has zero exfil
recovery opportunity here, regardless of the ranking method.

WHY THIS MATTERS
More packet/flow warnings do not automatically mean finding another data-theft
incident. Calling five-minute windows independent episodes would manufacture the
appearance of additional evidence. We retain the original unit and report the
ceiling instead. No new XAI superiority or novelty claim is supported.

FRESH-DATA STATUS
Korving sample: transfer direction does not establish outward exfiltration.
CIC 2021 official source: documented outward transfers, registration required;
registration details requested from the user, no personal information submitted.
CIC feature-only mirror: inspected header lacks time/capture/episode identifiers.
CIC 2024 derivative: not independent of its 2021 source captures.
AIT and UNRAVELED: previously exposed; useful development sources, not fresh tests.

NEXT GATE
Obtain and qualify official raw DNS-exfiltration captures. Verify timestamps,
transfer direction, episode boundaries, duplicate captures and benign traffic.
Before any new training, measure whether current telemetry misses whole episodes
and whether additional permitted evidence can recover them. If there is no such
headroom, do not rerun the XAI selector on that source.
The CIC source's existing uncertainty-triggered history method is a required
baseline, alongside AFA-SHAP, EDFA, and a capacity-matched ordinary-feature selector.
Any custom controlled collection would need matched benign transfers, independently
recorded outbound runs and a reserved test split. It would establish lab findings
only. It is not a substitute for claiming real-world APT efficacy.
