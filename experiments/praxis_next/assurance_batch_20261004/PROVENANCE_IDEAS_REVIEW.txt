REVIEW: FIVE XAI / PROVENANCE PRAXIS IDEAS
4 October 2026. Literature and design review; no new experiment results.

MAIN FINDING
The top two proposed gaps have close published precedents omitted from the draft.
Do not carry forward its "nobody has done this" language. A narrow comparative
application may remain possible, but changing the dataset alone is not sufficient.

1. NARRATIVE-TO-PROVENANCE GATE: HIGH OVERLAP
EviGuard, published 8 September 2026, already proposes provenance-grounded claim
compilation, deterministic checks, temporal/identity/policy relations, three-valued
verdicts and an action gate for industrial incident reasoning. This is direct
architectural overlap, not merely adjacent CTI-text research. Its public data
statement promises a later archival release; an accessible benchmark DOI was not
established in this review. Its reported results are not independently reproduced.
https://www.mdpi.com/2076-3417/16/18/8925
DOI: 10.3390/app16188925
Publisher-indexed full-text sections were accessible through search; direct page
downloads returned 403/429. Do not describe the data as downloaded or runnable.

Additional related methods: SHIELD, Auto-Prov, TRACE-CTI, FAX, and Reasoning
Externalization. The last two were already identified in our earlier review.
https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.10873
https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.17100
https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.24563

Data correction: DARPA TC releases logs and attack documentation; a ready, complete
label for every graph edge and every narrative assertion is not thereby established.
https://github.com/darpa-i2o/Transparent-Computing
Our UNRAVELED flow table is not automatically a process/file provenance graph.
Verify actual entity IDs, edge semantics, ordering/clock uncertainties and the
mapping to source attack documentation before claiming claim-level ground truth.
An absent event can mean incomplete observation; it does not prove the claim false.
Keep graph-supported facts, unknown claims and causal interpretations distinct.

Possible research question: under incomplete telemetry, which verified explanation
procedure retains useful incident evidence at a fixed review workload? This requires
explicit comparison with the closest gate, a template and a citation-only checker.
It remains a proposed question; neither novelty nor data adequacy is confirmed.

2. EXPLANATION DIVERGENCE AS MIMICRY WARNING: BROAD IDEA ALREADY STUDIED
Detecting adversarial evasion in deep learning intrusion detection systems using
explainable AI already defines SHAP drift and top-k overlap checks and learned
explanation-based detection. Its paired clean/adversarial construction is a material
limit for deployment, where the original clean counterpart may be unavailable.
https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s10207-026-01299-x
The research opportunity would have to go beyond that comparison: a detector that
uses only deployment-available evidence, valid attack executions and equal review
budgets. Feature-space changes alone cannot prove the attack still works.

Verified mimicry foundation:
Goyal et al., NDSS 2023, Sometimes, You Aren't What You Do.
https://www.ndss-symposium.org/wp-content/uploads/2023/02/ndss2023_f207_paper.pdf
Do not assume its reported success rates transfer to a new detector or dataset.
Compare explanation signals with score, input-drift/density and behavior-based
controls; retain all perturbation failures and unchanged-prediction cases.

3. STAGE-AWARE COUNTERFACTUALS: POTENTIALLY DISTINCT, HARD TO VALIDATE
ProvX already applies counterfactual explanation to provenance-based detection and
uses explanation feedback for robustness. The NIDS diffusion paper already studies
actionable counterfactuals and global filtering rules. Neither supports an automatic
claim that stage constraints are new.
https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.06073
https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.17161
A smaller defensible target is validity of an intervention against measured
protocol/causal prerequisites. An ATT&CK stage list is not a universal mandatory
linear execution order. Changing a prediction to benign can represent evasion,
not successful prevention. Evaluate what happened to the actual transfer/attack,
not only what happened to the model label.
This requires executable scenarios, observed prerequisites and completion evidence;
the current flow-only data cannot establish all of these. Defer as a simple Praxis.

4. PROGRESSIVE DISCLOSURE: HUMAN STUDY OPPORTUNITY, NOT AUTOMATIC NOVELTY
The current v2 of Too Much to Trust? includes the CORTEX framework and reports a
survey plus analyst interviews. Its v1 has a different title; pin the version.
https://arxiv.org/html/2503.02065v2
The cited January 2026 interface paper explicitly studies decision accuracy and
cognitive load as well as trust. The draft's "trust rather than performance"
distinction overstates the gap.
https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.22653
Measure correctly resolved incidents per unit time, missed attacks, wrong actions
and workload; do not maximize alerts/hour in isolation. Randomize matched cases,
account for repeated analyst/case observations and record task difficulty.
Tier-specific conclusions need enough people within each tier. A small team can
establish feasibility, not broad workforce benefit. Bot ratings cannot establish
human throughput or comprehension.

5. EXPLANATIONS FOR LABEL QA: CHEAPEST QUALIFICATION PILOT, NOT PROVEN NOVEL
There is existing IDS work on counterfactual-based contaminated-label correction:
X-NetIntrospector (2026).
https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S138912862600616X
Publisher search text supports the overlap; full-text retrieval was not available.
Do not equate a model-label disagreement with a labeling error.
Compare attribution-based review ranking with out-of-fold loss, confidence,
ensemble disagreement and established label-error methods, at equal review count.
The primary outcome is independently confirmed label errors found per review.

Best candidate source to qualify: original CICIDS2017 captures plus the published
correction pipeline and releases from Engelen et al.
https://intrusion-detection.distrinet-research.be/WTMC2021/
Their corrections affect flow construction/features as well as labels. Do not
join old and regenerated rows by row number or assume a one-to-one correspondence.
First establish shared packet/flow identities or a label-only correction subset.
Do not train on all corrected labels and call rediscovering the supplied corrections
new error detection. Keep adjudicated test corrections hidden from fitting/selection.
This is a dataset-QA study; CICIDS2017 is not an independent APT exfiltration study.

OTHER CLAIMS TO FIX IN THE DRAFT
- High accuracy alone does not prove a dataset artifact. Demonstrate leakage,
  duplicates, shortcuts or split sensitivity before attributing the result to them.
- ProvX is not the only relevant explanation work; our earlier CyberShapley
  full-text review also found APT explanation and analyst-facing evaluation.
- A graph edge records a represented relationship, not automatically maliciousness
  or successful data theft. Keep observational support separate from attack labels.
- A low unsupported-claim rate does not prove useful explanations: a system that
  says almost nothing can achieve it. Report claim coverage and essential omissions.
- Sampling bounds require the specified population and independent units. Related
  claims/flows from one incident cannot be counted as independent incidents.

FEASIBILITY ORDER
1. Label-QA qualification: smallest engineering pilot if trustworthy row identity
   and independently corrected labels can be established.
2. Narrow narrative consistency comparison: strongest thesis fit but direct prior
   art makes the contribution narrower than the attachment claims.
3. Deployable explanation-based mimicry detection: worthwhile only with valid
   attacks and a clean-counterpart-free inference rule; currently unqualified.
4. Human progressive-disclosure study: requires actual analysts and suitable tasks.
5. Stage-constrained counterfactuals: largest validity and execution burden.

RECOMMENDATION
Use this list to refine questions, not launch five supposedly novel studies.
First qualify the label-QA mapping and the narrative claim reference set. Compare
against the direct precedents before spending on another model batch. No new
experiment IDs were assigned or paid experiments launched for these five ideas.
