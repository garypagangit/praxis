PX-108 / PX-109: qualification decision, 4 October 2026

DECISION
STOP before CIC baseline or XAI fitting under the frozen successful-episode
protocol. This is a data-qualification failure, NOT a finding that the composite
baseline catches every attack and NOT a failed XAI hypothesis. Headroom remains
not evaluable. New AWS spending: $0. Model fits: 0.

WHAT WAS FROZEN BEFORE THIS CHECK
Commit c8deb0d contains the protocol and SHA-256 freeze record. Earlier archive
inspection is disclosed. The work is a prospective development test; it is not
an untouched-data preregistration. Go only with at least three missed verified
successful episodes at a 5% total-case budget, after selecting the strongest
non-XAI comparator on separate calibration executions. At least six verified
successful development episodes are required. This is a feasibility minimum,
not a power calculation. Do not weaken the model after observing a failure.

WHY THE SOURCE GATE DID NOT PASS
1. The publisher describes outbound DNSExfiltrator transfers and receiving/
   decoding files. This supports the intended attack behavior. The downloaded
   attack archives contain PCAP files only, with no per-run receiver completion
   logs, hashes, or independently linked successful-transfer manifest. That does
   not prove the original transfers failed; it limits what we can verify.
2. Capture labels and time ranges are not per-packet attack labels. For example,
   light_audio spans about 173 minutes, while the published audio attack interval
   spans 37 minutes. This duration discrepancy does not depend on timezone. It
   may reflect background capture outside the attack; it is not proof that every
   packet or every author label is wrong. Do not label the entire file malicious.
3. A direct question-section parser confirms the DNS limitation, independently
   of whether response/resource-record parsing succeeds:
                 IPv4 DNS queries   Complete questions   Incomplete/invalid
   Attack files       252,447               835                251,612
   Benign files       172,694           172,580                    114
   The earlier 84,000 decodable queries were from two BENIGN files. They cannot
   supply the missing attack names. Computing entropy on short retained prefixes
   would change the feature and could expose the capture mechanism. We did not
   zero-fill missing fields or silently drop the DNS comparator.
4. Valid IPv4 headers remain available in 1,124,896 attack-file packets and
   707,082 benign-file packets. This makes a header/timing-only observational
   study possible in principle. It does not establish episode labels or enable
   the complete DNS composite required by the current protocol.

ORDERING RESULTS
Across all 18 captures: 2,186 backward timestamp steps in 17 captures; stable
sorting moves 4,372 records. Attack files account for 1,291 steps across 11 of
12 captures. No exact timestamp+wire-length+packet-byte duplicate records were
found within a capture. This does not rule out near-duplicates or truncated-prefix
collisions. Exact duplicate archives were excluded separately.

The largest backward step is only 47 MICROSECONDS overall (30 microseconds in
attack captures). Do not characterize this as demonstrated large clock drift.
The frozen rule sorts by integer timestamp, then original record index, and
uses strictly earlier timestamps for history. All 18 ordering checks passed.
The number of independently verified episodes affected remains unknown because
the episode manifest is missing. Offline sorting is not proof of online behavior.

PROPOSAL CHANGES
- Strong baselines: LightGBM, logistic regression, anomaly, individual SOC
  heuristics, equal-weight percentile composite, and a learned full-feature
  selector. The composite includes byte asymmetry, outbound volume, complete DNS
  name length, and name entropy times query rate at matched review capacity.
- PX-108 primary intervention is a frozen pass/fail referral gate. Failure of
  the explanation check prevents automatic closure of a silent case; it never
  demotes an existing warning. Continuous ranking is secondary and labeled so.
- No purported causal-feature top-k ground truth is assigned from attack labels.
- PX-109 needs complete captures and sender/receiver hash equality under changed
  timing. Self-generated runs test controlled mechanisms and carry an explicit
  external-validity limit; they cannot establish efficacy on real APT campaigns.
- Published stateful CSVs are excluded. Any new history features come directly
  from timestamps and documented packets, not an unverified feature-table join.
