PX-108 / PX-109 amendment: qualification before headroom before XAI
Date: 2026-10-04. This amendment takes precedence over the initial candidate notes.

EXPOSURE AND SCOPE
Attack ZIP contents, counts, time ranges, truncation and DNS decoding summaries
were already inspected before this amendment. No CIC model has been fitted and
no CIC prediction/headroom results have been inspected. This is a prospective
development freeze, not an untouched-data preregistration or public registry.
Any confirmation claim requires a separately reserved set of independent runs.

GO / NO-GO (applied in this order)
G0: At least one defensible episode manifest with independent execution IDs,
outbound direction, start/end evidence, and transfer-completion evidence. A
capture filename or publisher's general description of the tool is insufficient
to verify a particular successful episode. Lack of provenance is NOT zero
headroom. It is NOT EVALUABLE, and fitting stops before headroom measurement.
G1: Required baseline inputs are observable without label-dependent missingness.
Required DNS length/entropy cannot be inferred from a truncated question name.
No zero filling, parse-failure flag, captured length or snap length as predictors.
If the complete composite cannot be evaluated, do not silently weaken it and
claim superiority to the strongest baseline. Header-only studies are separate.
G2: Proceed to the XAI pilot only if the calibration-selected strongest non-XAI
baseline misses at least THREE verified successful independent development
episodes at B = 5% of eligible cases. Require at least six verified successful
development episodes overall. N=3 is a minimal feasibility rule, NOT a power
calculation or a publication threshold. Freeze a powered confirmation design
after the pilot; do not treat three rescued episodes as sufficient proof.
G3: Do not change the operating point/model after seeing G2 failure. Report each
registered baseline, the selected winner, and missed counts for all methods.
If any registered alternative recovers all episodes, report that fact explicitly.
G4: PX-109 cannot proceed without an isolated dummy-file transfer arm with
receiver hash equality, start/end records and complete captures. Re-timing the
existing PCAPs is sensitivity analysis, not a validated successful transfer.

LABEL QUALIFICATION
Grade independently: author capture category; packet/flow membership supported
by documented endpoint/tool/session criteria; transfer completion independently
verified. Do not promote one grade to the next. Inspect archive documentation,
publisher schedule, capture bounds and available receiver logs/hashes. Unknown
packets remain unknown; never mark every packet in Attacks/ malicious. Check
duplicate archives, duplicate capture hashes and exact duplicate packet records.
Author schedules are evidence, not a license to shift clocks until labels fit.
Timezone conversions require documentation. Record mismatches without relabeling.

ORDERING AND FEATURES
Discard published stateful CSVs for this study. Do not join by row number.
Reconstruct features directly from packet observations. Preserve original record
indices. For offline replay, stable-sort within each capture by (timestamp in
integer microseconds, original record index). Equal timestamps retain capture
order. Do not repair clock resets by inventing timestamps or merge separately
captured clocks. Flag backward steps, maximum backward jump, moved rows and
affected capture IDs; episode counts affected remain unknown until labels qualify.
History uses strictly earlier timestamps. Build source-host five-minute cases
on UTC boundaries; emit at window close, with no later packets. No claim of
real-time arrival ordering or early warning follows from this offline sorting.
Keep capture groups separate in splits; all derivatives from an execution stay
together. Host addresses identify cases but never enter model features.
Retain repeated packets unless proven duplicate timestamp+bytes+wire length;
report a deduplicated sensitivity arm before adopting deduplication as truth.

STRONG NON-XAI COMPARATORS
At the same case budget include: LightGBM exfiltration probability; logistic
regression probability; anomaly score; individual operational heuristics; and
an equal-weight composite of benign-calibration percentile ranks for:
  1. outbound/inbound byte asymmetry per host window;
  2. complete DNS question-name length;
  3. complete-name entropy times query rate;
  4. outbound wire volume per host window.
Direction requires a documented monitored-host role. Original wire lengths and
validated IP lengths are distinct measurements; never call either payload bytes.
Also include a small learned full-feature selector with the same input evidence
and capacity as any learned XAI selector. Pick the winner by calibration episode
coverage, then fewer benign referrals, then lower CPU cost, then method name.
Do not choose a deliberately weak base model to manufacture misses.

BUDGET AND HEADROOM ENDPOINT
For headroom, B=5% of all label-independently constructed observed host/windows;
the count is ceil(0.05*n), separately on each evaluation partition. Each method
ranks these cases; deterministic ties use case IDs. This equal-budget offline
ranking is a feasibility comparison, not a deployed threshold guarantee.
Existing warning preservation is an independent later endpoint: first report
base warnings, then the additional 5% budget among previously silent cases.
Never mix total-budget headroom and additional-review-budget recovery counts.
Split complete verified executions into disjoint training, calibration and
development-evaluation groups before baseline fitting; publish the manifest.
Without those groups G0 fails. No row split or random seed supplies independence.

PX-108 THESIS FIT: PASS/FAIL REFERRAL, NOT AN UNDECLARED RANKER
Primary gate: a previously silent case passes only if its explanation has
supported evidence and a group-response fidelity score >= tau. Unsupported or
below-threshold cases fail automatic closure and become referral candidates.
Existing warnings are always retained. No rule demotes an attack warning.
Calibrate tau once on calibration runs to respect the additional 5% referral
budget; freeze it before evaluation. Overflow uses chronological FIFO and records
deferred cases and delay. Underfill stays underfill. Compare confidence and
composite threshold gates with the same queue policy and maximum capacity.
Continuous top-B scoring remains a secondary analysis, labeled XAI-as-feature.
Gate score: margin-response advantage of explanation-selected feature groups
over equally sized random groups under supported joint replacements. The exact
group/replacement manifest must be frozen after source qualification and before
fitting the XAI gate. Do not implement this gate on unqualified input.
There is no justified top-k causal-feature ground truth in CIC labels. A threshold
on an investigator's preferred feature list would not establish causality.
Novelty cannot rest on a threshold or deterministic wrapper alone.

RQ1/H1: At equal maximum added-review workload, does the frozen fidelity gate
surface more previously missed verified episodes than the strongest calibrated
non-XAI gate? H1 predicts a positive paired execution-level difference.
RQ2/H2: Does explanation content supply incremental benefit? H2 predicts reduced
benefit when attributions are removed/shuffled, with evidence, capacity and queue
rules held fixed. Compare a full-feature selector and prediction sensitivity.
RQ3/H3: Does benefit persist on independent executions with coherent replacements?
H3 predicts a positive paired difference there. Use execution-level uncertainty;
do not call seeds or thousands of packets independent replications.
