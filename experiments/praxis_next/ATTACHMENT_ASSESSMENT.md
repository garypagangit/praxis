# Assessment of the attached recommendations

The attachment correctly identifies measurement studies and artifacts as possible contributions even when a proposed model does not win. It overstates novelty, data readiness, guaranteed coverage, and certainty of academic acceptance. Result-independent value still requires a useful new question and credible evidence.

1. **Temporal evaluation:** execute a bounded companion study as PX-082. [TESSERACT (2019)](https://www.usenix.org/conference/usenixsecurity19/presentation/pendlebury) established temporal evaluation constraints in malware. Recent [Bilot et al. (USENIX 2025)](https://www.usenix.org/conference/usenixsecurity25/presentation/bilot) and [Guerra et al. (2026 manuscript, author-reported NDSS 2027 acceptance)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.01454) already examine substantial APT/provenance evaluation problems. Do not claim the general intersection is empty, that every APT benchmark can be qualified, or that a small measured difference certifies temporal robustness. Our controlled same-anchor test separates test-population changes from one part of the time-mixing comparison.
2. **Few-shot foundation models plus calibration:** the existing [tabular batch](../apt_benchmark/tabular_batch/README.md) and [follow-up](../apt_benchmark/tabular_followup/README.md) already implement much of this proposal. The literature review there identifies prior cybersecurity uses and calibrated variants. Rare-class support does not permit the entire proposed 32-to-1,024 labels-per-stage grid. Repeating these exposed-data fits under a new experiment number is not new independent evidence. Conformal coverage requires assumptions; adding conformal sets cannot guarantee coverage under arbitrary shift.
3. **Evidence-linked dataset:** a plausible future artifact contribution, but existence alone is insufficient. It needs independently verified execution outcomes, benign lookalikes, timestamps, linkage quality, licensing, and diverse independent runs. Our previous loopback artifact illustrates why a verified operation is not automatically verified malicious intent. PX-080/081 use qualified existing data first; a new multi-modal source is separately being qualified.
4. **S-DAPT-2026:** the [existing source audit](../apt_benchmark/sdapt2026/README.md) records withdrawal of the January arXiv manuscript and a lack of qualified downloadable author data. Paper-reported scenarios cannot be treated as acquired observations, and an unspecified corrected release cannot support a first-mover experiment. No invented substitute is labeled S-DAPT.
5. **Prompt-injection guarantees:** a separate application and research program. The attachment does not provide the claimed adaptive-study citation. No universal bound or novelty is inferred from that statement; outside the two requested APT method experiments.
6. **C2PA hardening:** also a separate engineering project requiring a named vulnerability, current implementation audit, and precise security property. Outside this APT batch.

The CTI archive remains valuable, but the completed external checker and revision results are substantive efficacy limits, not merely presentation problems. The [CTI decision](../../reports/cti_external_validation_20260918/DECISION.md) and [revision](../../reports/cti_checker_revision_20260918/REPORT.md) remain unchanged.
