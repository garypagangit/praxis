LIMITATIONS THAT MATTER BEFORE EXPANDING
- One custom generator on one machine; not an enterprise network or real APT.
- Separate runs share templates. No tool-independent or campaign-independent
  generalization is established, and repeated text payloads can be shortcuts.
- Base32-like benign telemetry and randomly encoded transfers deliberately
  overlap. For conditionally identical observations, a classifier or explanation
  cannot infer hidden intent. Missed-transfer headroom is necessary but NOT
  sufficient evidence of recoverability. Separate these controls before claiming
  that an explanation should rescue them; do not relabel or drop difficult runs.
- End-of-sequence features only; no claim of earlier detection or analyst benefit.
- The labels identify authorized laboratory transfer versus generated benign
  traffic, not real malicious intent. Controlled simulation must be explicit.
- Forty-eight/fewer rows in a split do not become many independent attacks merely
  because they contain many messages. Report run counts and uncertainty honestly.
- A full PX-109 test still needs verified timing changes and capture evidence at
  the declared network observation boundary. This pilot does not complete PX-109.
- Novelty remains unproven. Controlled transfer reconstruction already exists,
  for example https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.20423. Explanation monitoring and triage
  also have direct precedents cited in PROTOCOL.txt.
