PX-111: six XAI approaches compared in one fixed batch
4 October 2026

WHAT WE ARE DOING
We are testing whether explanation information adds measurable detection value
beyond ordinary model scores at the same investigation workload. A positive
result is possible but not required. We keep the entire planned comparison;
we do not repeatedly change methods, data or endpoints until something wins.
CIC failed target-row qualification. AIT failed recovery-headroom qualification.
PX110 established a controlled data pipeline. PX111 now tests six XAI candidates.

WHAT THESE SIX METHODS REPRESENT
These are six inexpensive attribution-based approaches, not an exhaustive test
of all explainable AI. They address hidden positive evidence, conflicting
evidence, stability, fit disagreement, explanation similarity and functional
fidelity. The batch cannot rule out future value from explanation-guided model
repair, intelligible rule learning, evidence retrieval or human investigation.
Those would need distinct hypotheses and fresh evaluation, not retrospective
renaming of the same negative result.

ONGOING RESEARCH RULE
1. State one measurable question and predefine a finite batch and strong controls.
2. Qualify the development source AND proposed external source for that endpoint.
3. Run all methods, retain failures, use multiplicity-aware summaries.
4. Freeze any selected candidate before testing fresh external recorded data.
5. Require the same benefit at comparable workload externally before claiming
   generalized recovery. Report separately whether evidence is controlled lab,
   independently recorded lab or operational organization data.
6. A synthetic-only win stays exploratory. A null batch is a result, not a reason
   to keep tuning until it wins. Human/SOC benefit requires its own evaluation.

DECISION
This batch does not support a positive XAI recovery contribution. Do not promote
any of A-F as a successful repair or spend AWS money expanding the same comparison
without a distinct justified mechanism and an eligible external dataset.
No paid compute used. The original audit/OR-gate findings remain separate.
