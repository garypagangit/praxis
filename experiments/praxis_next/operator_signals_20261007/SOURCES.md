# Sources and claim boundaries

This folder contains original analysis of existing public research data. It does not contain newly generated attacks or recruited participants. Raw Honey transcripts are kept in the ignored local cache, outside this publication.

* **Honey for the Agent: Cyber Deception and Behavioral Fingerprinting of LLM-Based Attackers**, Dario Maddaloni, Anastasia Safargalieva and Emmanouil Vasilomanolakis. [Versioned dataset](https://zenodo.org/records/20818246). The primary input is `Logs_final.zip`; the exact SHA256 is in `evidence/DATA_AUDIT.json`. The release documents command, shell-response, model and timing fields. Its timing descriptions are not treated as proof of comparable observer clocks. Existing research already covers model fingerprints and environmental deception; the full paper was not available in this review, so overlap with all its analyses remains unresolved.
* **Offensive Cybersecurity Time Horizons**, Jack Payne, Jeremy Miller and Sean Peters, Lyptus Research. [Research note](https://lyptusresearch.org/research/offensive-cyber-time-horizons), [public data and code](https://github.com/lyptus-research/cyber-task-horizons-data), pinned revision `178c0da1800f9c07311e1b8d6c61dd8104e7b4ae`. Data CC-BY 4.0; code MIT. B1 reuses our prior PX121 conservative derived shell records, with extraction limitations retained. This branch's inherited PX120/121 work includes those public-data derivatives and their source manifest. Shared tasks improve comparability but do not remove recorder/harness differences.
* **TRACE: Unmasking AI Attack Agents Through Terminal Behavior Fingerprinting**, Murali Ediga and Sudipta Chattopadhyay. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/html/2605.01186v1). Prior work for TF-IDF/SVC command fingerprinting, prefixes, argument removal, scaffold transfer and evasion analysis. We implement a TRACE-style baseline, not an exact TRACE replication on its original data. Those techniques alone are not novelty claims.
* **Who Is Behind the Harness? Fingerprinting LLMs through Agentic Behavior**. [LIDAR paper](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.28559v1). Verification, failure recovery and conflicting feedback already serve as active coding-agent probes. Our narrower candidate is passive output-conditioned recovery on archived cyber-operation logs, evaluated for incremental value and observation loss. A generic recovery-fingerprint claim would overlap.
* **Known By Their Actions: Fingerprinting LLM Browser Agents via UI Traces**, William Lugoloobi et al. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/html/2605.14786v1), [research repository](https://github.com/KabakaWilliam/known_actions). Adjacent passive behavior, early identification, timing perturbation/retraining and unknown-agent evaluation. These generic ideas are not first-ever contributions here.
* **LLM Agent Honeypot: Monitoring AI Hacking Agents in the Wild**, Reworr and Dmitrii Volkov. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/html/2410.13919v2). Timing-based attacker screening predates this work. Its potential field detections are not independently verified labels for our binary experiment.
* **Observational Human-AI (OHAI)**, Dustin D. Updyke, David Rossell and Shelly Fitzgerald, CMU SEI. [Framework](https://sei.cmu.edu/library/observational-humanai-ohai-a-defender-attribution-framework-for-distinguishing-human-vs-ai-threats/). Probabilistic attribution and evidence quality already motivate this field; a confidence flag alone is not a new contribution.
* **OpTC**, DARPA/Five Directions and collaborators. [Original release](https://github.com/FiveDirections/OpTC-data). A possible separate APT-telemetry fallback; not used in these experiments and not a source of AI-versus-human operator labels.

Candidate novelty must be a specific new empirical finding or validated engineering method beyond these precedents. A short literature review cannot establish exhaustive priority. No guarantee of committee acceptance or publication is implied.

Numerical implementation reference: [scikit-learn 1.5.2 LinearSVC documentation](https://scikit-learn.org/1.5/modules/generated/sklearn.svm.LinearSVC.html). `dual` selects the primal or dual optimizer; the final correction retains the same loss, penalty, regularization and class weights. SVC decision margins are not probabilities.
