## Closest-work novelty assessment

**PX-080:** The implemented change is a supervised regressor selecting between current and context classifiers from forward-fold error differences, with an illustrative class weight of four for movement/exfiltration. It is a cost-sensitive expert-selection adaptation, not evidence of a new general ensemble principle. StageFinder already studies temporal/structural APT stages; KnowHow already uses contextual attack-step reasoning. Bilot et al. support strong simple controls. None of these citations establishes that this exact loss-and-intervention combination is unoccupied, and the measured tradeoffs do not establish method superiority.

**PX-081:** The implemented policy greedily ranks two optional groups using predicted error reduction divided by cost, with simulated deadline and availability handling. Guney et al., SEFA and Learning-To-Measure already provide learned acquisition methods; Sim-CTKG places cost and latency constraints directly in cybersecurity telemetry selection. The pilot changes the practical target and replay conditions. It neither reproduces those systems nor resolves Learning-To-Measure's dynamic-feature limitation, because feature values remain precomputed.

**PX-082:** Temporal and distributional evaluation constraints predate this study in TESSERACT; Guerra et al. directly examine contemporary PIDS benchmarking. Its defensible contribution is a specific, reproducible same-anchor sensitivity measurement and documented stage/alert tradeoffs on qualified data. Additional cross-dataset evidence could strengthen that empirical contribution, but neither a first temporal-audit claim nor an exhaustive novelty guarantee is justified.
