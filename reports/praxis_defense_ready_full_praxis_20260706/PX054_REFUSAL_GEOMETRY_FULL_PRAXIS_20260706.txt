Abstract of Praxis
This praxis characterizes refusal-style geometry in Huginn-0125, a recurrent-depth language model. A source gate confirmed public, ungated, Apache-2.0, Transformers-compatible recurrent-depth access. An activation smoke gate captured 60/60 latent rows across depths 4, 8, 16, and 32. The scale gate captured 600/600 rows across 120 safe prompts and depths 4, 8, 16, 32, and 64, with cross-depth stability 0.9257, CI [0.9067, 0.9273], benign-control FPR 0.0000, and worst refusal TPR 0.9750.

Keywords: mechanistic interpretability, recurrent depth, refusal geometry, Huginn, latent states
