Abstract of Praxis
This praxis evaluates a safety-gated no-label test-time adaptation strategy for streaming APT stage detection. The defended result shows that a locked selective BatchNorm adaptation policy improved Macro-F1 from 0.7685 to 0.8658 and Reconnaissance F1 from 0.0250 to 0.5050 while preserving Data Exfiltration F1 and limiting overrides to 0.0470. A seven-seed defense extension preserved the conclusion, while DAPT2020 remains an external-validity boundary.

Keywords: test-time adaptation, APT detection, safety gate, source shift, intrusion detection

CHAPTER 1: INTRODUCTION

1.1 Background
Safety-Gated Test-Time Adaptation for Streaming APT Detection is part of the Praxis Research portfolio, a future-work-to-experiment program for AI/ML and cybersecurity engineering. The portfolio standard is evidence-first: a result is promoted only when it has a defined objective, a reproducible artifact trail, a measured gate, and a written claim boundary.

1.2 Research Motivation
Security operations need detectors that can adapt to stream shift without allowing adaptation to damage high-risk attack classes. PX-001 targets that practical tension by combining TTA with an explicit deployment safety gate.

1.3 Problem Statement
Unlabeled target streams can expose rare-stage failure modes, but naive online adaptation can introduce unsafe prediction changes. The problem is to recover rare Reconnaissance detection without sacrificing Data Exfiltration stability.

1.4 Thesis Statement
A streaming intrusion detector can use tightly gated, unlabeled test-time adaptation to recover weak early-stage attack detection while preserving high-risk Data Exfiltration behavior under a locked source-file shift.

1.5 Research Objectives
1. Measure the frozen support-floor detector under held-out source-file shift.
2. Apply a validation-selected selective TTA policy without test-label tuning.
3. Evaluate whether Reconnaissance and Macro-F1 improve under locked replay.
4. Audit whether high-risk Data Exfiltration predictions remain protected.

1.6 Research Questions and Hypotheses
RQ / Hypothesis | Statement
----------------+-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
RQ1 / H1 | Can no-label TTA improve rare-stage detection under source-file shift? Hypothesis: Locked selective TTA improves Macro-F1 by at least 0.05.
RQ2 / H2 | Can a selective safety gate preserve Data Exfiltration behavior? Hypothesis: Locked selective TTA improves Reconnaissance F1 by at least 0.25.
RQ3 / H3 | Is the gain distinct from simply rejecting uncertain predictions? Hypothesis: Locked selective TTA does not reduce Data Exfiltration F1.
RQ4 / H4 | Does the effect persist across a defense hardening replay? Hypothesis: The override rate remains bounded at or below 0.05.

3.9 Evaluation Metrics and Statistical Methods
Metrics:
- Accuracy = correct predictions / all predictions.
- Precision_c = TP_c / (TP_c + FP_c).
- Recall_c = TP_c / (TP_c + FN_c).
- F1_c = 2 * Precision_c * Recall_c / (Precision_c + Recall_c).
- Macro F1 = arithmetic mean of per-class F1 across the evaluated stages.
- Recon F1 = class-specific F1 for the Reconnaissance stage.
- DE F1 = class-specific F1 for the Data Exfiltration stage.
- PR-AUC = area under the precision-recall curve; reported as a diagnostic rather than the headline claim because the observed delta is only +0.0006.
- Override rate = count(prediction changed by gate) / total test rows.
- Changed-from-DE count = number of frozen Data Exfiltration predictions overwritten by the adaptation/gate path.

Acceptance thresholds:
- Macro F1 delta >= +0.05.
- Recon F1 delta >= +0.25.
- Mean DE F1 delta >= 0.0000.
- Per-seed DE F1 delta >= -0.05.
- Override rate <= 0.05 for the locked replay.
- Confidence-reject matched-rate control must not explain the Recon gain.

Model and gate math:
- Let f_theta(x) be the frozen support-floor MLP.
- Let BN_phi denote BatchNorm running statistics adapted on the unlabeled target stream with model weights theta fixed.
- Let y_frozen = argmax f_theta(x) and y_tta = argmax f_theta,BN_phi(x).
- The gate g(x) chooses y_tta only when validation-selected rescue conditions are met and protected DE conditions are not violated.
- Final prediction: y_hat = g(x) * y_tta + (1 - g(x)) * y_frozen, where g(x) is a deterministic Boolean decision implemented by the locked policy.
- The safety objective is lexicographic: recover Recon when possible, but do not trade away protected Data Exfiltration behavior beyond the registered safety floor.

3.10 Validation Approach
Validation uses five defenses against overclaiming:
1. Locked replay: policy is selected before final test replay and is not tuned on test labels.
2. Leakage audit: source-file overlaps are zero and temporal delta state is reset per split.
3. Null comparison: frozen confidence rejection at the same rejection/override rate leaves Recon F1 at 0.0000.
4. Defense hardening: additional seeds, stream-order sensitivity, override decomposition, and PR operating-point diagnostics are preserved.
5. Boundary evidence: DAPT2020 TTA is negative and remains a limitation, not a hidden failed replication.

3.11 Assumptions
Source-file split approximates deployment stream shift; Data Exfiltration protection is a higher-priority safety constraint than unconstrained macro optimization. The method assumes the unlabeled target stream is representative of the deployment stream and is not actively poisoned. Adversarial stream poisoning remains future work.

4.5 Cross-Experiment Analysis
- Accuracy improved from 0.8984 to 0.9243.
- Macro-F1 improved from 0.7685 to 0.8658.
- Reconnaissance F1 improved from 0.0250 to 0.5050.
- Data Exfiltration F1 remained stable, 0.9157 to 0.9202.
- PR-AUC changed only from 0.8732 to 0.8738, so the correct interpretation is an operating-point/gate improvement rather than a broad representation-ranking improvement.
- Matched-rate frozen confidence rejection did not recover Reconnaissance, so the improvement is not explained by merely abstaining on uncertain frozen outputs.
- The validation split has unusually high Recon support; this strengthens the motivation for a shift-aware gate but prevents IID-generalization claims.
- DAPT2020 is negative for TTA transfer and must remain in the defense as external-boundary evidence.
