**Internal AI-assisted decision material — September 15, 2026.** This is a contribution screen, not submission prose or an academic approval. **Recommendation: retain as the strongest completed bounded empirical alternative; novelty remains unproven.**

## Problem and research question

Accurate test records can give an incomplete picture of a proposed code revision. Does changing which genuine execution records a reviewer sees increase harmful acceptance, and can independent test acquisition reduce harm while retaining useful repairs?

The candidate contribution is a controlled disclosure-policy estimate with fixed program pairs, reserved executable outcomes, matched acquisition budgets, useful-repair controls, and a generated-proposal boundary check. Public HumanEvalPack/HumanEvalPlus supply 164 tasks; qualification admits 135 native pairs, including 101 heldout problems. Supplier acquisition is capped at 16 observations and disclosure at two. These are finite-suite outcomes on public tasks with possible training exposure.

## Evidence and limits

- Schema-constrained Qwen accepts **12/101 harmful native revisions under selected disclosure versus 4/101 under uniform disclosure**: +7.92 percentage points, task-bootstrap 95% interval [2.97, 13.86], exact one-sided p=.00390625, Holm-four p=.015625. Paired counts are four both, **eight selected-only**, zero uniform-only, and 89 neither. Useful acceptance is 100/101 in both arms.
- The treatment combines passing-record filtering, omission/fewer records, and pairing. Exploratory inspection finds withheld uniform-arm failures in seven of eight additional acceptances; two selected messages contain no records. It does not isolate persuasion, pairing, or a causal effect of schema constraints; original Qwen heldout decisions were never collected.
- Devstral's 3/101 versus 1/101 contrast is unsupported (adjusted p=.75). Its native observations are reused from V1, not fresh replication.
- The **hybrid defense fails**: Qwen enforced harmful acceptance is 4/101 versus uniform verification's 3/101; Devstral is zero in both. Useful retention cannot rescue failed harm reduction.
- Generated transfer contains only **34 harmful deliberate corruptions**, below the 40-task adequacy threshold: selected/uniform acceptance is 4/34 versus 4/34 for Qwen and 1/34 versus 1/34 for Devstral. These are not naturally occurring errors.
- The archived audit passed 1,099,300 checks. Its disclosed erratum changes a secondary approximately 4.08e-19 effect to exact zero/no directional benefit; primary hypotheses remain unchanged. Audit success does not certify novelty.
