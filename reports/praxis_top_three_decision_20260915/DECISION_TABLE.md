## 010: preserve the existing D0 decision gates

Source: [existing protocol](/C:/w/px010_dev/final_praxis/010_development_20260915/D0_PROTOCOL.md) and [machine specification](/C:/w/px010_dev/final_praxis/010_development_20260915/D0_SPEC.json). These are proposed investment margins, not literature-derived guarantees.

1. **Technical validity first.** Clean-repeat discrepancy above `1e-5`, nonfinite outputs, incomplete identities, changed protected inputs/targets or independent-channel displacement above `max(1e-5, 10 × maximum clean-repeat discrepancy)` means **HOLD FOR DIAGNOSIS**. A software defect is not evidence of cross-channel harm.
2. **Mechanism gate.** In raw joint forecasting, at least two of the six fixed perturbation variants must each produce displacement of at least `0.05` standardized units in at least `8/32` contexts, exceeding the numerical floor, and mean off-channel error inflation of at least `0.02` over **all 32 contexts**. Otherwise **HOLD CROSS-CHANNEL SCALE-UP**. Retain all variants and unfavorable findings.
3. **Simple-control gate.** If either clipping or causal SMA-5 reduces mean off-channel error inflation to at most `0.005` in every raw-joint qualifying variant, while increasing clean off-channel mean absolute error by at most `0.01`, **HOLD THE COMPLEX DEFENSE**. Independent forecasting is the structural negative control in D0; later utility comparisons must address its clean-performance tradeoff.
4. **Only if both scientific gates favor further work:** design D1 with fresh natural multivariate telemetry, explicit sensor-trust assumptions, matched baselines, clean performance and recovery endpoints, independent incidents, uncertainty and a justified sample size. D0 uses one previously inspected series and overlapping semisynthetic contexts; it supplies no independent cyber confirmation.
