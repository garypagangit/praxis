# 010: Cross-channel forecast harm — provisional contribution brief

**Internal AI-assisted research planning · September 15, 2026.** Qualification is complete; D0 is unrun, its executable runtime is unfrozen, and no novel defense result exists. This brief supports research decisions and preserves that distinction.

## Problem, question and candidate contribution

A corrupted sensor history might influence forecasts for otherwise unchanged sensors in a joint forecasting model. **RQ:** Does modifying one channel create measurable off-channel forecast error, and does the effect survive simple preprocessing?

The unproven contribution is a controlled measurement of cross-channel harm in native TimesFM 3, potentially motivating directional access to trusted histories. Q1 established executable feasibility and baseline comparisons; its three-channel shift did not isolate this mechanism. A later defense would need natural multivariate telemetry, defensible trust assumptions, and fresh retention/recovery evaluation.

## Fixed D0 decision gates

The [existing protocol](/C:/w/px010_dev/final_praxis/010_development_20260915/D0_PROTOCOL.md) specifies **32 overlapping contexts**, six step/ramp perturbations of channel 0, and four pipelines: joint, independent, joint with clipping, and joint with causal SMA-5. Each uses its own clean forecast. Including clean repeats gives **4 × 32 × (1 + 6 + 1) = 1,024 evaluations**. Artificial channels share one previously inspected public series; these are descriptive development measurements.

Spillover is mean absolute forecast displacement over unchanged channels 1–2. Error inflation averages their perturbed-minus-clean absolute errors. A qualifying variant requires raw-joint spillover **≥0.05** in **≥8/32** contexts, exceeding the numerical floor, and mean error inflation **≥0.02**. Both conditions must hold in the same **≥2 variants**. Variant means use **all 32 contexts** equally; clean MAE uses **64 targets**, never only high-spillover cases.

The floor is max(1e-5, 10 × maximum clean-repeat discrepancy). Clean-repeat discrepancy **>1e-5**, or independent spillover **>floor**, means operational HOLD. Audit saved arrays, unchanged inputs/targets, hashes, finite outputs and all assignments.

Failure of the mechanism gate holds scale-up. If **either** preprocessing baseline achieves mean inflation **≤0.005 in every qualifying variant** with clean-MAE increase **≤0.01** versus raw joint, hold the complex defense. Otherwise, passing permits separate D1 design only.

Execution first requires a committed worker, runtime freeze and checked controls. The existing proposed cap is **one host, 30 minutes, $10 combined reserve**, with stop protection and verified shutdown; this brief spends nothing and grants no external authorization.

## Five skeptical defense questions

1. **Is displacement harmful?** Require error inflation against unchanged targets.
2. **Are 32 contexts independent replications?** No; overlap and one source limit inference.
3. **Who guarantees protected sensors?** D0 assumes protection; deployment must justify it.
4. **Why not separate forecasts or clipping?** Structural controls and the explicit baseline gate constrain complexity.
5. **What proves originality and practical benefit?** Neither qualification nor D0 suffices; closest-prior comparison and later controlled evaluation remain necessary.
