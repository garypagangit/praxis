## Setup

Target detector: `RandomForestClassifier`

Dataset: CIC-IDS2018, sampled uniformly at `700` rows per file.

Membership protocol:

- Members: sampled training rows used to fit the detector.
- Nonmembers: held-out `02-03-2018` rows.
- Attack scores: max confidence, negative entropy, true-class probability, and correctness.

## Interpretation

This is a real positive privacy signal, but it is not yet a standalone Praxis lead. The train/nonmember accuracy gap is large, so the confidence attack may partly measure ordinary overfitting plus day-shift rather than trace-specific memorization. That is still valuable: the final paper can ask whether APT detector training traces leak under realistic temporal shift, but the next version must control for calibration and distribution shift.

## Recommended Next Step

Run a 3-seed shadow-model protocol on the strongest existing Unraveled/CIC detector family, with matched nonmember rows from the same temporal/source-file distribution where possible. Promote only if attack ROC-AUC stays above `0.60` after calibration and distribution-matched controls.

## Praxis Candidate Flag

Possible backup candidate, not the lead. The signal is good enough to continue, but TTA remains the stronger new Praxis candidate because it already has a direct performance gain and reproducible AWS run.
