What remained promising

  • Policy-1 stopped accuracy rose in all three cells.
  • Mean generated-token saving exceeded the 20% utility target in every cell.
  • The first policy prevented 46–71% of observed overthinking events.
  • Gate 2 remains a valid bounded discovery result.

Why certification stopped

  • The harm ceiling was 2%; observed rates were 5.6–8.0%.
  • Certification required p ≤ 1/60; each p-value was effectively 1.
  • Fixed-sequence testing must stop at the first failure.
  • No policy could be selected, so holdout generation was prohibited.
