## 1. Purpose and research question

Gate 2 established a bounded positive result on one 200-item GSM8K sample,
Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct, and one eight-round iterative reconsideration protocol.
It did not establish cross-model or cross-domain behavior.

H4 asks a narrower follow-on question:

> Can the same registered policy family be **calibrated separately within three
> fixed model-domain cells**, using only each cell's calibration sample, so that
> the selected policy receives an exact finite-population early-stop-harm
> certificate and subsequently meets preregistered held-out point gates for
> harm, accuracy, and compute?

H4 is a study of **risk-controlled calibrated adaptation**. Because a separate
policy is selected in each cell, it is not a zero-shot transfer test of the
Gate 2 threshold and must not be described as one.

The design is motivated by the operational reversal phenomenon described in
[When More Thinking Hurts](https://aclanthology.org/2026.findings-acl.1199/),
the multiple-risk-testing framework in
[Learn then Test](https://arxiv.org/abs/2110.01052), and the broader
risk-control framing of
[Conformal Thinking](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.03814). These references
motivate the design; they do not pre-establish an H4 result.

There is no OpenBookQA fallback. Its 500-row test split cannot supply the
registered 500 calibration plus 300 held-out rows. There is no model or corpus
fallback in Revision 2.1.

## 4. Frozen split construction

Each corpus receives one calibration split of 500 rows and one held-out split
of 300 rows. The remaining eligible rows are unused:

| Corpus | Population | Calibration | Held out | Unused |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| GSM8K after Gate 2 exclusion | 1,119 | 500 | 300 | 319 |
| ARC-Challenge test | 1,172 | 500 | 300 | 372 |

Split construction is deterministic and occurs before model collection:

1. Load the pinned source bytes and verify the source SHA-256.
2. Construct stable question IDs from the pinned source.
3. For GSM8K, verify and remove all 200 Gate 2 question IDs.
4. Rank eligible rows by
   `SHA256("<seed>:<question_id>")`, ascending, with `question_id` as the
   deterministic collision tie-break.
5. With calibration seed `5701`, select the first 500 rows.
6. Remove those rows. With held-out seed `5702`, rank the remainder and select
   the first 300 rows.
7. Use the single resulting ARC calibration/held-out partition in both C2 and
   C3.
8. Write explicit JSONL split files and a split-freeze manifest containing row
   counts, IDs, source revisions, source hashes, split hashes, seeds, and the
   selection algorithm.
9. Commit the split files and freeze manifest before any scientific generation.

The exact finite-population tests below treat the hash-ranked selection as the
registered without-replacement randomization mechanism. The estimand is the
harm proportion over the named eligible finite population under the pinned
execution protocol. No inference to arbitrary math, science, user, or
production traffic is permitted.

## 5. Trace protocol

Each question receives eight sequential rounds:

- greedy decoding (`do_sample=False`);
- maximum 256 generated tokens per round;
- one frozen, domain-neutral H4 initial-solution/reconsideration template that
  preserves Gate 2's iterative structure but is not byte-identical to the
  arithmetic-specific Gate 2 prompt;
- numeric final-answer normalization for GSM8K;
- deterministic choice-letter normalization for ARC;
- fixed option order from the pinned ARC source;
- no gold answer or correctness signal exposed to the model or stopping policy.

Within H4, only the answer-format instruction changes by corpus. Prompt wording
is otherwise identical across all three H4 cells. Exact prompt-template bytes
and hashes are pre-data locks. Invalid, missing, or out-of-vocabulary
answer extractions are recorded as the empty normalized answer and scored
incorrect. They are never repaired by reading the gold label.

Confidence is
`exp(mean normalized transition log probability)` over generated, non-padding
tokens, including the generated EOS token if present and excluding all prompt
tokens. The value is clipped to `exp([-20, 0])`. This is a sequence-level
mean-token-probability proxy, not calibrated correctness probability.

For each round, the evidence corpus preserves the raw prompt, raw response,
normalized answer, confidence, generated-token count, cumulative generated
tokens, wall-clock seconds, and GPU-seconds. Generated tokens remain the primary
compute measure; timing measures are descriptive.

## 6. Frozen policy family

The 30 candidate policies are the full factorial grid:

| Parameter | Values |
|---|---|
| Minimum stopping round, `m` | 2, 3, 4 |
| Answer-window length, `k` | 1, 2 |
| Confidence threshold, `tau` | 0, 0.02, 0.05, 0.10, 0.20 |

At round `t >= m`, a policy qualifies when:

1. the last `k` normalized answers are identical; and
2. if `tau > 0`, every one of the last `k` confidence values is at least
   `tau`.

The first qualifying round is returned; otherwise round 8 is returned.

`k=1` is intentionally a **no-repeat control**: a one-answer window is
vacuously stable. Therefore `(k=1, tau=0)` is a fixed stop at round `m`, not an
answer-stability policy. For `k=2`, two consecutive normalized answers must
match. `tau=0` disables the confidence condition. The Gate 2 adaptive policy is
`(m=2, k=2, tau=0.05)`.

No policy, threshold, grid value, extraction rule, or qualification rule may
change after the pre-data freeze commit.

## 7. Calibration-independent fixed sequence

The fixed-sequence order is determined entirely by policy parameters and is
independent of calibration outcomes. Policies are ordered lexicographically:

1. `m`: 4, 3, 2;
2. within `m`, `k`: 2, 1;
3. within `(m,k)`, `tau`: 0.20, 0.10, 0.05, 0.02, 0.

The resulting immutable sequence is:

| Index | Policy | Index | Policy | Index | Policy |
|---:|---|---:|---|---:|---|
| 1 | `(4,2,.20)` | 11 | `(3,2,.20)` | 21 | `(2,2,.20)` |
| 2 | `(4,2,.10)` | 12 | `(3,2,.10)` | 22 | `(2,2,.10)` |
| 3 | `(4,2,.05)` | 13 | `(3,2,.05)` | 23 | `(2,2,.05)` |
| 4 | `(4,2,.02)` | 14 | `(3,2,.02)` | 24 | `(2,2,.02)` |
| 5 | `(4,2,0)` | 15 | `(3,2,0)` | 25 | `(2,2,0)` |
| 6 | `(4,1,.20)` | 16 | `(3,1,.20)` | 26 | `(2,1,.20)` |
| 7 | `(4,1,.10)` | 17 | `(3,1,.10)` | 27 | `(2,1,.10)` |
| 8 | `(4,1,.05)` | 18 | `(3,1,.05)` | 28 | `(2,1,.05)` |
| 9 | `(4,1,.02)` | 19 | `(3,1,.02)` | 29 | `(2,1,.02)` |
| 10 | `(4,1,0)` | 20 | `(3,1,0)` | 30 | `(2,1,0)` |

This is a conservative-priority lexicographic sequence. Policies that differ
on several parameters are not assumed to be monotonically ordered by true harm
or compute. The sequence is not inferred from observed token saving, harm,
accuracy, or p-values, and fixed-sequence validity does not require monotonic
risk.

## 8. Primary exact finite-population risk control

For cell `c`, policy `lambda`, and question `i`, define:

`L_i(lambda) = 1`

if the round-8 answer is correct and the selected early-stop answer is
incorrect; otherwise `L_i(lambda) = 0`.

Let:

- `N_c` be the eligible finite-population size;
- `M_c(lambda)` be the unknown number of harmful questions in that population;
- `R_c(lambda) = M_c(lambda) / N_c`;
- `n = 500` be the without-replacement calibration sample;
- `x_c(lambda)` be observed calibration harms;
- `alpha = 0.02`;
- family confidence-failure budget `delta_family = 0.05`;
- per-cell testing level `delta_cell = 0.05 / 3 = 1/60`.

For each policy, the null is:

`H0: R_c(lambda) >= 0.02`.

Because the population is finite, the least-harmful null boundary is:

`M0_c = ceil(0.02 * N_c)`.

Thus `M0=23` for C1 and `M0=24` for C2/C3. The primary p-value is the exact
lower hypergeometric tail:

`p_c(lambda) = P[X <= x_c(lambda)]`,

where `X ~ Hypergeometric(N_c, M0_c, 500)`.

The lower-tail probability is monotone under the composite null, so evaluating
the boundary gives the valid worst-case p-value. The earlier Revision 2
binomial calculation is retained only as an i.i.d. sensitivity analysis; it is
not the H4 primary test.

Within each cell, start at policy 1 and test at `delta_cell`. Certify a policy
only if its p-value is at most `1/60`. Stop permanently at the first failure.
Policies after the first failure are **not reached** and are not described as
failed. This preregistered fixed sequence controls within-cell family-wise
error at `delta_cell`. Allocating `0.05/3` to each of the three cells controls
the three-cell family error at no more than 0.05 by the union bound.

The certified set is the reached certified prefix. If it is non-empty, select
`lambda-hat` from that set by:

1. greatest unrounded calibration mean generated-token saving;
2. then greatest prevention rate on calibration overthinking events;
3. then lowest calibration harm count;
4. then prefer `tau=0`;
5. then earliest fixed-sequence index.

If no calibration overthinking event exists, prevention rate is treated as
negative infinity for tie-breaking. This affects only policy selection, not
certification.

If the first test fails, the registered conclusion is that this fixed sequence
produced no certified prefix at the registered sample size and error budget.
It is not a claim that every grid policy violates the 2% bound.

## 9. Global calibration-to-holdout lock

All calibration work is completed before any held-out model generation:

1. Commit the preregistration, code, exact environment manifest, config, prompt
   files, source manifest, explicit split files, frozen audit-selection rule,
   and their SHA-256 manifest.
2. Submit exactly one calibration job per cell. Immediately write, commit, and
   push its launch ARN, request hash, source version, source hash, and result
   prefix before that cell's result may be fetched or evaluated. A retry under
   this experiment ID is forbidden.
3. Collect all three 500-question calibration trace corpora.
4. Fetch only the registered jobs by exact S3 object VersionId; verify every
   downloaded byte hash; commit and push each collection and cloud manifest.
5. Verify exactly 4,000 unique question-round generations per cell and 12,000
   total calibration generations.
6. Run the registered exact finite-population LTT procedure independently in
   C1, C2, and C3.
7. Write each full 30-policy determination, including the fixed order, every
   p-value, reached/certified flags, selected policy, input hashes, and code
   commit.
8. Write three external calibration lock manifests that hash the determination,
   registered launch, cloud retrieval manifest, calibration traces, raw
   generations, split, config, prompts, environment, and source code.
9. Commit all three lock manifests and determinations. Record the remote Git
   commit SHA.
10. Only after the holdout runner verifies all three committed locks and their
    launch/cloud protected artifacts may any held-out model response be
    generated.

File creation times and modification times are not lock evidence. A held-out
runner must refuse to execute when an expected file is uncommitted, dirty,
missing, hash-mismatched, or associated with a different Git commit.
Pre-generating or informally “unlocking” held-out traces is prohibited.

No scientific source, code, model, prompt, extraction rule, split, or
calibration determination may change between the freeze and held-out phases.

The Revision 2.2 Phase A and calibration launcher/fetch paths mechanically
enforce pushed Git identity and version-specific, downloaded-byte SHA-256
evidence for source, runtime, cloud-evidence, collection, and SageMaker model
artifacts. Calibration source bytes are retrieved by the registered S3
VersionId and hash-checked before extraction or execution. The holdout phase
must receive the same external retrieval treatment before a final H4 evidence
package can claim S3 immutability for held-out artifacts.

## 10. Held-out point gates

Each frozen `lambda-hat` is applied once to its cell's 300 held-out traces.
The following are preregistered point-estimate gates:

| Gate | Exact criterion | Interpretation |
|---|---|---|
| H4a | Certified prefix non-empty on calibration | Exact finite-population certificate exists for the selected cell policy |
| H4b | Held-out early-stop harm count `<= 6/300` | Empirical harm-consistency point gate |
| H4c | Selected correct count minus fixed-long correct count `>= -3/300` | Accuracy point gate, equivalent to at least -1 percentage point |
| H4d | Unrounded mean generated-token saving `>= 0.20` | Compute-saving point gate |

H4b–H4d are not additional confidence certificates. In particular, H4b is not
the source or a formal validation of the calibration certificate. A failure of
H4b is a held-out empirical inconsistency; it is not, by itself, proof that the
LTT certificate was false, that exchangeability failed, or that distribution
shift occurred.

Overall H4 scientific success requires H4a, H4b, H4c, and H4d to pass in all
three cells. Cell-level outcomes are also reported separately. No averaging
across cells can rescue a failed cell.

## 11. H4e confidence-component diagnostic

H4e is an operational component-selection diagnostic, not a scientific pass
gate and not a deployment authorization.

If all three cells have a selected certified policy, confidence is marked
`RETAIN_FOR_FUTURE_PX057_CANDIDATE` only when `tau > 0` in at least two of the
three selected policies. Otherwise it is marked
`RETIRE_FROM_FUTURE_PX057_CANDIDATE`. If any cell has no selected certified
policy, H4e is `INCONCLUSIVE`.

The diagnostic says only whether confidence survived this preregistered
certificate-and-saving selection rule. It does not establish calibration,
causal value, an accuracy contribution, or production readiness.

## 12. Blinded manual extraction audit

The audit selection rule is frozen before held-out generation. After held-out
responses exist, select 50 held-out trace IDs per cell by ascending
`SHA256("5703:<question_id>")`, with `question_id` as the collision tie-break.
Because C2 and C3 share the ARC held-out split, this rule selects the same 50
ARC question IDs for both cells; their model outputs are audited separately.

Every selected trace contributes all eight round outputs, for exactly 400
manual extraction judgments per cell. The reviewer receives the question,
frozen choices where applicable, and raw response, then independently records
the answer expressed by the response. The reviewer does not receive the gold
answer or automated extracted answer. The reviewer is blinded to:

- automated extracted answers and correctness labels;
- selected stopping policy and stopping round;
- policy p-values and certificate status;
- H4b–H4d metrics and decisions.

Manual extraction judgments are locked before comparison with the automated
extractor. A second
blinded adjudicator resolves only judgments the primary reviewer marked
ambiguous; the final manual judgments are then hashed and committed before the
held-out gate is adjudicated.

The registered software workflow is:

1. `prepare_px057_h4_manual_audit.py --export-packet` writes a packet containing
   only the question, choices when applicable, raw response, and blank manual
   fields.
2. The primary reviewer fills every answer field without access to gold,
   automated extraction, policy, certificate, or point-gate information. A
   second blinded adjudicator fills only rows marked ambiguous.
   `<NO_ANSWER>` is the required sentinel when no final answer is extractable;
   the join maps it to the registered empty normalized answer.
3. The completed blinded judgment file is committed.
4. `prepare_px057_h4_manual_audit.py --join` verifies that commit and creates
   the automated/manual comparison artifact.
5. The comparison artifact is committed. The point-gate runner hard-fails
   before computing H4b–H4d unless that committed audit is complete and valid.

The extraction audit passes with at most 8 disagreements out of 400 (2%) in each
cell. Nine or more disagreements void the cell's scientific gates. A failed
audit cannot be repaired after viewing the held-out gates and then rerun on the
same held-out data. Extractor repair requires a new preregistration and a fresh
held-out evaluation; the original cell remains reported as invalid/descriptive.

## 13. Completeness and outcome-independent adjudication

The adjudicator independently recomputes source rows, split membership, gold
answers, policy outcomes, exact statistics, selection, and scientific gates
without importing gate decisions. It intentionally reuses the canonical
Git-lock and manual-audit schema validators used by the execution runners; those
provenance/schema checks are therefore replicated checks, not a fully
independent implementation. A fully external reproduction remains required
before describing provenance validation as independently implemented.

The adjudicator must recompute or verify:

- pinned source, model, tokenizer, prompt, environment, config, split, and code
  identities;
- GSM8K Gate 2 exclusion and split disjointness;
- ARC split reuse across C2/C3;
- 500 calibration and 300 held-out IDs per cell;
- eight ordered rounds per trace;
- 4,000 calibration and 2,400 held-out generations per cell;
- the exact 30-policy order;
- every finite-population p-value and fixed-sequence stop;
- each `lambda-hat` and every deterministic tie-break;
- all H4a–H4d integer/unrounded gates;
- H4e as an operational diagnostic;
- every 50-by-8 manual-audit comparison;
- the global calibration-lock commit preceding any held-out generation;
- SHA-256 and immutable object-version evidence.

External SHA-256 manifests hash exact file bytes. A JSON file does not claim to
contain its own hash; its hash is stored in a separate manifest. The manifest
records repository-relative path, byte length, SHA-256, Git blob/commit, and
cloud object version where applicable.

Any missing row, duplicate ID, incomplete trace, source mismatch, dirty lock
file, or temporal lock violation makes the affected cell protocol-invalid.

## 14. Registered negative-result handling

- **H4a empty prefix:** report that the registered fixed sequence did not
  produce a certificate; held-out data for that cell are not generated.
- **H4b failure:** report the empirical held-out inconsistency without calling
  it a certificate violation or inferred distribution shift.
- **H4c failure:** retain the calibration certificate, withdraw the accuracy
  preservation claim for that cell.
- **H4d failure:** retain the calibration certificate, withdraw the compute
  saving claim for that cell.
- **Manual-audit failure:** mark the cell invalid and descriptive only.
- **Lock/integrity failure:** stop; do not regenerate or repair after viewing
  scientific outcomes.

No threshold, grid value, split, model, corpus, policy order, audit threshold,
or claim boundary may be changed to rescue a negative result.

## 16. Compute envelope

The registered maximum is:

- 3 cells × 800 questions × 8 rounds = **19,200 generations**;
- 19,200 × 256 = **4,915,200 maximum generated output tokens**;
- 12,000 calibration generations and 7,200 held-out generations;
- approximately **12 times** the Gate 2 maximum generation count/token budget,
  not four times.

Actual generated tokens may be lower because EOS can occur before 256 tokens.
Cost, duration, and GPU-hours are planning estimates only and do not alter the
protocol.

## 17. Claim boundary

If and only if H4a–H4d pass in all three cells and every integrity/audit check
passes, the permitted claim is:

> On the named finite GSM8K and ARC-Challenge benchmark populations, under the
> pinned models, prompts, decoding settings, and execution environment,
> separately calibrated stopping policies received exact finite-population
> early-stop-harm certificates with a three-cell family error budget of 0.05
> and met the registered held-out point gates for harm, accuracy, and generated
> tokens.

Even after a full pass, H4 does not support a claim of:

- zero-shot threshold transfer;
- arbitrary cross-model, cross-domain, or large-scale robustness;
- risk control on distributions other than the named finite populations;
- calibrated confidence probabilities;
- production safety, deployment readiness, latency, energy, or dollar savings.

Before results exist, the only permitted statement is:

> H4 Revision 2.2 is designed to seek an exact finite-population certificate.
> No H4 certificate or H4 scientific result currently exists.
