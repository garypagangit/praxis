What worked

  • Adaptive accuracy was 35.6% versus 33.0% fixed-long.
  • The policy added 13 correct answers.
  • Early-stop harm was exactly at the 4-of-500 ceiling.
  • Collection, cloud, request, source, code, and bundle integrity passed.

What rejected the mechanism

  • Mean saving missed the threshold by 7.27 percentage points.
  • 1,708 of 4,000 rounds were malformed under the exact schema.
  • Four sentinels had no valid answer; one was valid but wrong.
  • All gates were required; favorable averages cannot override failures.

Claim boundary

The pilot may support only a development decision about this exact mechanism on exposed H4 C1 data. It is not H5 calibration, holdout, certification, confirmation, cross-model transfer, cross-domain transfer, deployment evidence, or large-scale robustness. It does not show that adaptive stopping generally fails.
