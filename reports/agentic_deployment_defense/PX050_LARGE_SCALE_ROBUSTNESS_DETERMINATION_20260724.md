# PX-050 Large-Scale Robustness Determination

Date: 2026-07-24

## Determination

**Positive, with a bounded large-scale claim.**

PX-050 may claim large-scale robustness for the repaired deterministic gate within:

- the original paper artifact's frozen PyPI and npm registries;
- the supported pip, npm, pnpm, and yarn command grammar;
- the six preregistered direct, pinned, manager-variant, mixed-package, flagged, and shell-chain families; and
- the preregistered synthetic absent-package mutation process.

It may not claim universal install safety, detection of malicious packages that already exist, arbitrary-shell robustness, or direct replay of the paper's withheld 576,000 outputs.

## Transparent experiment sequence

### PX-050W discovery

The preregistered first run processed 1,000,000 commands. It observed zero invalid allows, but the overall protocol status was **FAIL** because eight npm names beginning with `--` were interpreted as flags and routed to review:

- four safe-valid rows;
- four absent-package rows.

This was retained as a real parser finding rather than erased.

### Repair

The parser was changed from “discard every token beginning with a hyphen” to an explicit allowlist for the two valueless flags generated by the protocol. A replay of the discovery corpus then passed all registered checks. Because this replay followed outcome inspection, it is repair evidence, not the independent confirmation.

### PX-050X independent confirmation

PX-050X was registered with a fresh seed before inspecting its outcomes and processed another 1,000,000 commands.

## Defensible claim language

> On an independently seeded, preregistered corpus of one million package-install commands constructed from the original study's frozen registries, the repaired PX-050 gate produced zero invalid allows across 500,000 absent-package cases (one-sided 95% upper bound 0.000599%), allowed 100% of 416,668 supported safe-valid commands, and blocked 100% of 166,664 shell-chain cases. These results support large-scale robustness within the tested command grammar, registry snapshot, and mutation families.

## Claim boundary

This is a large-scale **gate robustness** result, not a universal software-supply-chain result. The public paper artifact withheld the original prompt/output-level hallucination evidence for security reasons, so the study does not claim a direct replay of those 576,000 generations.
