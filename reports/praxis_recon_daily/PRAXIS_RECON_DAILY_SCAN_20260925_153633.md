> Mounting geopolitical frictions, trade disputes and public health emergencies have exposed structural fragilities in global supply chains, yet the tools built to anticipate those fragilities still keep relational reasoning and adaptive decision-making apart. This paper joins them. We assemble a multimodal supply chain knowledge graph spanning 1.86 million entities and 23 relation types drawn from input–output tables, customs records, corporate disclosures and a curated disruption case repository, then design an attention-augmented R-GCN encoder that compresses topology, attributes and temporal disruption signals into resilience-sensitive embeddings. On that representation the proposed KG-PPO algorithm couples a clipped policy objective with cascade-aware reward shaping, so that interpretable transmission paths emerge alongside the resilience forecast.
