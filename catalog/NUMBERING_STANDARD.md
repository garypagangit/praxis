# Pagan Praxis Numbering Standard

Effective: 2026-09-23

The public-facing portfolio uses **Experiment 001, Experiment 002, ...** as the permanent identifier.

## Mapping rule

- Historical `PX-001` through `PX-065` map directly to **Experiment 001** through **Experiment 065**.
- Historical **Final Praxis 001** maps to **Experiment 066**.
- Historical **Final Praxis 002** maps to **Experiment 067**.
- Historical **Final Praxis 003** maps to **Experiment 068**.
- The next new research candidate is **Experiment 069**.

Historical IDs are never deleted from source reports. They remain provenance aliases so old citations, filenames, hashes, manuscripts, and experiment logs remain valid.

## Naming rule for new work

Use:

`Experiment NNN — Short Descriptive Title`

Recommended canonical directory for new experiments:

`experiments/experiment-NNN-short-slug/`

Development branches may use:

`experiment-NNN-short-slug`

but the permanent scientific record should ultimately be linked from the catalog rather than depend on a moving branch.
