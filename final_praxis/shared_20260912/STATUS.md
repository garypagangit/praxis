**The strongest comparative evidence.** With reference explanations, Devstral's gate recovered 22/44 initial errors, versus majority 30/44 and unanimity 27/44; harms were 0/84, 2/84 and 1/84. Qwen recovered 11/51 versus 16/51 and 12/51; harms were 1/77, 2/77 and 1/77. Neither met the required harm reduction while preserving recovery against both controls. The controls had little observed harm, limiting attainable benefit; this does not convert a failed screen into a positive result. Equal call counts also did not mean equal cost: the proposed checks consumed more tokens.

**What remains worth investigating.** The broader question of containing a corrupted specialist still fits the user's interests, but it is a research direction, not a selected project. A new candidate must first show a specialist making a measurable useful contribution, then demonstrate containment across different fault families while retaining that contribution and beating permanent ablation, random routing and ordinary verification. If those prerequisites cannot be demonstrated, investigate other ideas rather than extending these implementations. Repairing the current baselines alone would not establish novelty.

**Why the literature matters.** [Extended Refusal v2](https://arxiv.org/html/2505.19056v2) already tests benign fine-tuning retention; 005 is reproduction/feasibility, not a new resistance result. [MiCRo v3](https://arxiv.org/html/2506.13331v3) already studies ablation and steering. [SRU](https://arxiv.org/html/2608.26511v1), [SWAY](https://arxiv.org/html/2604.02423v1) and [Self-Blinding](https://arxiv.org/html/2601.14553v1) constrain novelty claims for counterfactual revision. Public data used include [GSM8K](https://github.com/openai/grade-school-math), [HarmBench](https://github.com/centerforaisafety/HarmBench), [XSTest](https://github.com/paul-rottger/exaggerated-safety), [ARC](https://huggingface.co/datasets/allenai/ai2_arc), [SOCKET](https://huggingface.co/datasets/Blablablab/SOCKET) and SRU's released EX-FEVER material. Immutable source/data pins and licensing notes remain with each protocol.

**Interpretation limits and preserved failures.** The studies are small feasibility tests. 005 uses one seed and one model family, same-size adaptation, automated judges, and substantial output truncation; math gains partly coincide with shorter answers. 006 uses synthetic faults and a tiny-backbone MiCRo checkpoint whose empathy classification failed. Empathy labels proxy self-reported concern rather than objectively establish emotional understanding. 007 retains benchmark keys and their semantic limitations. Devstral uses the original parser; Qwen uses a separately frozen inline-format amendment, with calibration replay lineage preserved. These are separate protocols, not a pooled confirmatory replication. No failed result or original endpoint was rewritten. Future changes require a prospective protocol, refreshed closest-prior review and untouched test data.
