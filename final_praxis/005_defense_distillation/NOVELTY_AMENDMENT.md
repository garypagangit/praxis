# Post-launch novelty amendment

Recorded 12 September 2026 during baseline evaluation, before the first LoRA training stage. **Final Praxis 005 is reproduction and feasibility work. The user's publishable-novelty criterion is not met by the generic retention question.**

The base paper's [v2, dated 7 October 2025](https://arxiv.org/html/2505.19056v2), already compares ER and parent models after benign Dolly15k fine-tuning in section 4.5 and identifies residual drift and verbosity limitations. Our initial review cited v1 and missed this expansion.

The authorized pilot continues with every original hypothesis, example, setting and endpoint preserved. Its short mathematical adaptation and ordinary replay control can establish implementation capability and a limited replication/extension result. A positive outcome alone cannot establish novelty.

Before any subsequent model study, freeze a separate protocol and review the closest compression, cross-family transfer and defense-objective literature. A different student architecture or dataset alone would not establish a contribution. Any proposed new objective must identify what it adds beyond existing preservation methods and ordinary replay.
