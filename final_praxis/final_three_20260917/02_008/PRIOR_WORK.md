## Candidate contribution statement

> On a fixed, publicly reconstructible code-revision cohort, a prospectively specified passing-only disclosure policy increased harmful acceptance for one schema-constrained reviewer. Complete records identify omission and changed evidence count as parts of that policy, preserve a second reviewer's unsupported contrast, and show that the proposed independent-acquisition intervention failed its recommendation. Secondary generated revisions do not exhibit the native disclosure contrast.

This is an **empirical characterization and boundary study**. Its potential value lies in separating authenticated outcomes, supplier acquisition, disclosed evidence, independent tests, and reserved labels, and then measuring the resulting decisions. It does not depend on claiming that the simple hybrid score is a successful new algorithm.

The eight selected-only Qwen acceptances must remain eight paired observations: seven coincide with withholding a uniform failure, and two include empty selected testimony. The latter two are part of the seven, not additional cases. The finding therefore concerns the compound policy, not eight demonstrations of persuasion by passing examples. H1, the failed hybrid H2, and the generated null contrasts should appear together in any presentation.

## Questions a contribution defense must answer

1. Why is this paired intervention informative beyond showing that tests can be cherry-picked? A defensible answer must identify the controlled information boundaries, measured model-specific effect, complete negative comparisons, and reproducibility limits.
2. Why does an unsuccessful intervention still merit an applied research report? The answer must concern what the completed evidence teaches, rather than relabeling the hybrid as successful.
3. Does the program require a successful engineering method, or can it accept a rigorous empirical evaluation of an existing threat? The saved evidence does not decide this institutional requirement.
4. Are the finite benchmark outcomes meaningful for the intended practice? Any deployment recommendation should acknowledge function-level scope and the absence of a real-workflow validation.
5. What comparison would resolve a remaining originality objection? A focused citation comparison should precede a new experiment; additional significance on the exposed cohort would not establish originality.
