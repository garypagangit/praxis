## Closure is not a universal negative

The 004 result rejects the tested repair scaffold's capability prerequisite, not the general possibility of specialist corruption. The 005 result is a short-update, one-family automated-label finding, not evidence of safety or a test of every distillation attack. The 006 effect supports a limited logic-module contribution under its scorer, while failed completion gates prevent promotion. The 007 result rejects the added label gate under the registered contrasts, not every form of selective revision.

The 005 automated review is complete: strict V2 reached consensus on 23/28 cases; a separately labeled mechanical citation normalization reached 26/28 without changing labels or making calls, retaining two disagreements. The original human criterion remains unmet. No pending human review is invented, and automated agreement is not called human adjudication.

The 004 follow-up reused four exposed issues. The 006 follow-up used training questions and designated raw formatting diagnostic only. The 007 models used different parser lineages. These differences must remain visible if any earlier result appears in a paper.

## FalseCite-Code: useful positive with important limits

The historical identifier PX-004 is not the same experiment as the current branch Final-Praxis-004. Its positive result concerns fabricated software-artifact citations checked against external package/repository metadata. The current code-study threat concerns genuine passing tests selected to omit contrary evidence. Successful authenticity verification does not resolve evidence-selection bias.

The primary audit's strict-labeled panel had seven fabricated and eight clean cases. The base overblocked one clean case; the metadata and verifier conditions overblocked none. The verbose160 generation result used the same 45/20/15 split pattern as earlier prompt/length variants, with one failed parse on the strict panel. Its 5/6 fabricated-trust fraction is a parseable-case rate; all seven assigned fabricated cases and the unparseable outcome must remain visible. Do not describe the later prompt variant as fresh confirmatory evidence without a separate exposure audit.

Cross-model limits are material: general Qwen2.5-3B rejected too broadly in audit mode; the Phi attempt produced invalid verdict outputs; and Qwen2.5-Coder-3B generation trusted all seven strict fabricated cases even with metadata evidence, while the deterministic verifier rejected them. Registry checks share their truth basis with benchmark labels, so a perfect deterministic verifier on this slice is not broad semantic-verification evidence.

The June publishing package and short-paper Markdown are real local artifacts. Their wording includes "published," but no external submission, peer review, venue acceptance or external publication was verified here. A final user-facing status should say **local paper package exists**, not **externally published**.

## Contribution to the next paper

These studies support methodological lessons: establish a useful baseline before measuring preservation; distinguish refusal from safe utility; enforce completion and failure accounting; compare reduced harm with lost recovery; validate the outcome oracle; and separate authentic evidence from representative evidence. They are research history and appendix material. A new primary claim requires the current code study's own literature distinction, frozen causal design and results.
