# Paper framework and claim boundaries

**Current state: framework only; no new code-study results or primary-paper claim are asserted here.** The earlier campaign supplies feasibility lessons and reproducible negative results. It is not a pooled test of the present hypothesis.

Working question: Can a controller's independently acquired, task- and edit-dependent checks reduce acceptance of harmful code revisions supported by selectively disclosed truthful evidence while preserving useful repairs under the same checking budget?

The title, abstract and contribution list must be written after the actual acquisition policy and its closest-prior distinction are frozen. Do not promise a new defense merely from the existence of hidden failing tests. The intended artifact can distinguish a reproducibility package, a benchmark-qualification result and a hypothesis-supported research paper; these have different readiness requirements.

## Proposed manuscript structure

1. **Problem and threat:** define the initial program, proposed revision, specialist's information, truthful evidence-selection capability, controller access and reserved evaluator. State whether candidates are released canonical/buggy fixtures, generated repairs or adaptive attacks.
2. **Closest prior and contribution:** compare the exact mechanism against query-policy contracts, regression-test selection, coverage/mutation approaches and counterexample generation. Identify what the new policy computes that those alternatives do not, and an ablation that tests that difference.
3. **Method:** give the acquisition/acceptance rule, budget accounting, failure and abstention behavior, and all information boundaries. If theory is supplied, make its assumptions match the evaluated threat and test distribution.
4. **Benchmark qualification:** pin HumanEvalPack/HumanEval+ sources, resolve compatibility, report the complete assigned cohort, and distinguish reserved-suite passing from program correctness. Describe original-problem splits and dependence among languages/variants.
5. **Experimental design:** define corruption and repair transitions, honest and adversarial proposal generators, same-candidate paired comparisons, matched budgets, primary effect/recovery margin and uncertainty. Identify any pilot-exposed problems and keep them outside confirmatory evaluation.
6. **Results:** show full denominators, effect sizes and intervals, not just attack success among hand-selected favorable cases. Separate fixture qualification, specialist capability, primary intervention, ablations and sensitivity analyses. Keep negative and incomplete outcomes visible.
7. **Limitations:** finite test oracles, small informative cohorts, shared source problems, public-benchmark contamination, source access assumptions, test-generation cost and the difference between a fixed and adaptive adversary.
8. **Reproducibility:** frozen commits, source hashes, isolated runner, manifests, executable automatic review, raw trace custody, resource limits and cost receipts. No manuscript table should require an unavailable manual judgment.

## Role of earlier experiments

The prior 004–007 implementations and the AutoDCWorkflow audit justify design choices: qualify a useful specialist first; distinguish refusal from safe usefulness; treat completion limits as part of measurement; prevent selective updating from winning through blanket rejection; and validate the outcome oracle before inference. They did not evaluate the current acquisition policy.

Their samples, models, interventions and measurement protocols differ. Do not pool their counts, p-values, confidence intervals or success flags. The 004 source-access follow-up reuses exposed issues. The 006 completion-format pilot uses training questions and cannot replace the failed held-out format. The two 007 models have different scoring-parser lineages. The 005 human criterion remains unmet despite a completed automated replacement. Preserve those distinctions in any appendix.

The appendix evidence inventory records each study's exact artifact hash, source branch, outcome and admissible interpretation. Its function is transparent research provenance, not a sequence of independent replications or a proof that the final hypothesis was prespecified before the earlier trials.

## Results placeholders that must remain empty until measured

- Qualified problem counts by bug category and split.
- Honest generated-repair yield and useful correct-to-wrong / wrong-to-correct opportunities.
- Adversary screening cost, truthful-witness eligibility and baseline attack acceptance.
- Primary policy's paired harm reduction and repair-retention difference at a fixed budget.
- Closest-prior and component ablation comparisons.
- Automatic review verdict, remaining limitations and paper-development readiness.

A failed or underpowered study can still support a precise negative-results report if the question and measurement are worthwhile. It must not be relabeled as successful evidence for a novel defense to satisfy a desired paper outcome.
