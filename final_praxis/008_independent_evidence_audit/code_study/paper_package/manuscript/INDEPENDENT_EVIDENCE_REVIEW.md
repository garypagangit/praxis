# Independent evidence and citation review

Review date: 2026-09-14 UTC. Reviewer: the separately assigned `oracle_review` agent. This is an internal evidence review, not external peer review, a novelty certification, or an independent replication. No model calls, cloud jobs, candidate execution, or frozen-source edits were performed for this review.

**Disposition: the completed evidence supports a bounded empirical manuscript about selective disclosure.** The reported primary results agree with the public records and completed audits. The positive finding belongs to schema-constrained Qwen on native code pairs. A successful hybrid defense, a geometric-pairing mechanism, and general transfer are unavailable claims. Statistical significance does not settle whether the empirical increment over close prior work meets a venue's novelty standard.

This targeted check found no basis for a “first” claim. Exact-phrase searches for selective/cherry-picked executable evidence did not identify an identical study, but null search results cannot establish novelty. Its novelty is incremental: most diagnostic flips accompany omission of direct counterevidence, and the current design cannot attribute them to geometric clustering or sophisticated adversarial optimization.

## Required manuscript wording and remaining editorial actions

1. **Describe the whole disclosure treatment.** Use “selective disclosure, including withholding failed tests and possibly displaying no records.” Avoid “matching two passing tests causes the eight flips,” “fixed displayed evidence count,” or “geometric clustering attack.” A uniform-passing, fixed-count ablation is future work and was not run.
2. **Preserve version lineage.** V2 was frozen before heldout scientific inspection after development-only formatting diagnosis. It is a distinct schema-constrained Qwen configuration, with exact Devstral/native reuse. No V1 Qwen heldout comparison exists to establish that schema constraints caused vulnerability. “Preregistered” should be explained as the recorded pre-execution protocol/source freeze; do not imply an external registry registration that has not been documented.
3. **Use final methods identities.** The methods plan intentionally retains old prospective passages and a pre-freeze V2 hash. The completed manuscript should cite final protocol SHA256 `9e6ba43fd988b47273c13ae4a5dc569640d210d2178103afaab964ed2bf236c3` and extension freeze `9fbc377a01caa82665b4248fc542c18d8b94ebb521732de554936ab919808bc7`. Do not copy “no extension freeze asserted” or pending qualification language as current status.
4. **Keep finite-oracle qualifications visible.** “Harmful” means current passes H and proposed fails H under the frozen comparison/time limits; “useful” is the reverse. H is disjoint from the experimental tool inputs, not proven absent from training or free of semantic duplicates. Public benchmark recognition, curated qualification exclusions and managed-model backend changes limit generalization and future bitwise model reproduction.
5. **Report H2 prominently as unsuccessful.** The observed Qwen direction does not favor hybrid, but the one-pair difference is not a registered proof that hybrid is worse. No generated subgroup, veto effect, stable useful acceptance, or small offline contrast substitutes for the failed native primary endpoint.
6. **Treat the exploration bound as a conditional standard calculation.** It assumes a fixed finite pool, fixed failure statuses, sampling without replacement and completed observations. It does not imply a failure exists in A, establish H/general-input correctness, or prove hybrid superiority. Uniform sampling has the stronger simple worst-case finite-pool guarantee.
7. **Distinguish audit layers.** The initial failed V2 audit and amendment remain part of the result. Exact frozen-analysis reexecution establishes numerical reproducibility; independent artifact checks establish the bounded provenance/accounting contract. Neither is an independent model replication, semantic proof, external peer review or proof of novelty. The arithmetic helper and amendment have disclosed authorship overlap with the research team.

These are manuscript constraints and provenance corrections, not authorization to alter completed measurements or add favorable conditions to this heldout study. No further experiment is required to write the bounded completed-results paper.
