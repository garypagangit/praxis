# Non-CTI Praxis portfolio review — 2026-09-14

The strongest alternative to the completed 008 study is **PX-055, refusal-related geometry under quantization**, if the priority is a mechanistic model thesis. **PX-004 FalseCite-Code** and **PX-001/Praxis06 selective test-time adaptation** are the next credible alternatives, with substantial qualifications. None of these currently offers an unambiguously stronger combination of independently measurable outcomes, completed validation, novelty, and fit to the user's interests than **008's narrow selective-disclosure result**. This is a judgment about the available evidence, not a guarantee of publication.

I would finish the bounded 008 paper, retain PX-055 as the principal alternative, and avoid restarting the failed PX-057 stopping claim. A successful defense or new mathematical method remains an open research objective; neither 008 nor the strongest historical alternatives has already established that broader claim.

## 1. PX-055: strongest mechanistic alternative

The latest [independent R3 adjudication](C:/Users/garyp/OneDrive/Documents/codex/reports/refusal_direction_quantization/e1_e4_20260831/closeout_r3/PX055_R3_INDEPENDENT_ADJUDICATION.json) reports **H1_PRECISION_INVARIANT_BOUNDED**, with all **9/9 required model–precision cells**, three model families, and no integrity errors. Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct, Llama-3.1-8B-Instruct, and Gemma-2-9B-it were evaluated at FP16, bitsandbytes INT8, and NF4. Geometry pairs cover **116/116 safe-text rows per model**. The independent adjudicator recomputed quantities from source artifacts rather than consulting the production summary.

Across **70 effective quantized-layer cells**, the registered low-cosine, high-participation, and rank-spread fractions are all zero; the maximum reported principal angle is **15.4266 degrees**. Across **140 directed transfer ratios** on 35 effective layers, the minimum is **0.975** and the pooled ratio of means is **0.999096**. These support the registered bounded invariance claim. They are not 140 independent model replications. The behavior–geometry relationship is descriptive with only three model clusters, and **E4_PROXY_NOT_POSITIVE** holds for all three models. E4 used transient safe-text activation projection, not the original perturb-then-quantize checkpoint sequence, so it cannot establish the original H4 restoration claim.

The public behavioral base is [XSTest](https://github.com/paul-rottger/xstest/blob/main/readme.md), with 450 locally pinned rows. The geometry corpus, however, is a locally constructed set of safe refusal-style and benign statements. The actual [R3 runtime](C:/Users/garyp/OneDrive/Documents/codex/reports/refusal_direction_quantization/e1_e4_20260831/closeout_r3/cloud_receipt/code/run_px055_e1_e4_r3.py) normalizes output and marks refusal when a listed phrase occurs. Consequently, the current evidence is strongest for **style-related geometry and lexicon-defined behavior**, not semantic safety or a demonstrated refusal-control mechanism. Model revisions, data snapshots, runtime code, and artifact hashes are retained, making this substantially more reproducible than an anecdotal intervention study.

Novelty requires care. [Arditi et al.](https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.11717) already established a single-direction refusal intervention across models, and [Chhabra and Khalili](https://arxiv.org/abs/2504.04215) directly studied compressed-model refusal through mechanistic interpretability. The local [source-clearance assessment](C:/Users/garyp/OneDrive/Documents/codex/reports/refusal_direction_quantization/gate0_20260711/PX055_GATE0_SOURCE_CLEARANCE_20260711.md) itself flags that overlap. A claim to be the first quantization/refusal-direction study would be indefensible. The completed contribution is the specific controlled cross-family, cross-precision measurement and its limitations; planned GPTQ/AWQ calibration studies were not completed by this run.

**Defensible next RQ:** Does a direction estimated from refusal-style text predict independently judged refusal behavior across model families and precisions, after controlling for lexical style? **Candidate hypothesis:** high geometric transfer alone is insufficient to predict semantic refusal or preserve benign utility. A worthwhile extension would separate style and behavior with independently labeled examples, disjoint direction-estimation and outcome sets, and matched causal controls. Freeze those choices before examining new held-out outcomes. This is a proposed research direction, not a finding or established novelty claim. It needs a focused comparison with the compressed-refusal literature before funding additional runs.

Compared with 008, PX-055 has stronger architectural breadth and closer alignment with the user's model-modification interests. It has weaker outcome construct validity and a more direct prior-art collision. It is the best alternative if those gaps are acceptable, not an already proven defense awaiting a paper.

## 2. PX-004 FalseCite-Code: strongest small applied positive

The [paper-ready summary](C:/Users/garyp/OneDrive/Documents/codex/reports/falsecite_code/FALSECITE_CODE_SHORT_PAPER_20260628.md), [audit results](C:/Users/garyp/OneDrive/Documents/codex/reports/falsecite_code/FALSECITE_CODE_MODEL_GATE_SUMMARY_20260624.json), and [generation results](C:/Users/garyp/OneDrive/Documents/codex/reports/falsecite_code/FALSECITE_CODE_GENERATION_GATE_VERBOSE160_SUMMARY_20260625.json) cover **80 constructed software-reference claims**: 20 each for GitHub repositories, GitHub tags, NPM versions, and PyPI versions. There are 40 valid and 40 fabricated claims, split by artifact group into 45 training, 20 validation, and **15 strict holdout** rows: eight valid and seven fabricated.

For the main Qwen2.5-Coder-7B audit, the base model accepts **6/7 fabricated holdout claims**; the deterministic metadata guard accepts **0/7**, while allowing **8/8 valid claims**. Across all 80 rows, base acceptance is 30/40 fabricated claims. In the verbose-160 generation gate, **5/6 usable fabricated holdout responses** suggest trust, with the seventh fabricated assignment unparsed. The guard rejects all seven fabricated assignments and allows all eight valid ones. Thus the often-quoted 83.33% to 0% comparison has different usable denominators; the unparsed assignment must remain visible.

A strong metadata-evidence prompt also achieves **0/7 fabricated acceptance and 8/8 valid acceptance** on the main 7B model. The deterministic guard therefore does not beat that baseline on this holdout. The 3B coder boundary case is informative: its metadata prompt still accepts fabricated claims, while the guard rejects them. Other model conditions include overblocking and protocol failure and should not be pooled as interchangeable successful replications. The [source-verifier refresh](C:/Users/garyp/OneDrive/Documents/codex/reports/falsecite_code/defense_refresh_20260630/FALSECITE_CODE_SOURCE_VERIFIER_GATE_20260623.md) preserves the narrow scope.

The underlying repository/package metadata is public and can be snapshotted. Nevertheless, the 80-claim benchmark is a local adaptation, **not replication using the original FalseCite dataset**. This falls short of the user's ideal of starting directly from the base paper's public benchmark. Furthermore, the truth labels and guard use the same metadata facts: perfect agreement establishes a useful consistency check, not independent semantic verification. Existence/version validity does not establish package safety, feature compatibility, or completed coding-task correctness. Seven fabricated held-out claims cannot support a population-level zero-error claim.

The [FalseCite paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.11167) already studies fabricated-citation-driven hallucination, so “citations can mislead models” is not new. A defensible extension would ask whether independently sourced, time-pinned software evidence reduces erroneous decisions on disjoint package families while preserving useful behavior, compared with a full-metadata prompt and an abstention policy. It would need a substantially larger benchmark, independent outcome adjudication, stale/conflicting metadata cases, and downstream task outcomes. A novel uncertainty or decision rule would need its own predeclared test; the present deterministic lookup is not a new mathematical method.

Compared with 008, this is simpler to explain and deploy, but the sample is much smaller and its outcomes are less independent of the intervention. I would not move primary effort from 008 to FalseCite solely because the headline guard error is zero.

## 3. PX-001/Praxis06: strongest adaptation result with an explicit failure boundary

The [May final report](C:/Users/garyp/OneDrive/Documents/codex/reports/tta_streaming_apt/PRAXIS06_PAPER_READY_FINAL_REPORT_20260513.md) documents a substantial Unraveled result across three locked seeds: mean macro-F1 **0.7685 → 0.8658**, with stored unrounded mean delta **+0.0974**; Recon F1 **0.0250 → 0.5050**; and DE F1 **0.9157 → 0.9202**. The test split contains **65,869 flows from 25 source files across five capture days**. A matched-rate confidence-rejection control gives Recon F1 zero. The override rate is **4.70%**, but PR-AUC changes only **+0.0006**, suggesting an operating-point benefit rather than a broad improvement in ranking quality. Source/group overlap checks were zero; adaptation resets at each split.

The winning procedure combines unlabeled batch-normalization adaptation with a validation-selected selective gate. It should not be described as an implementation of every component of [Tent](https://arxiv.org/abs/2006.10726), whose established method includes entropy-driven optimization. The [Unraveled author repository](https://gitlab.com/asu22/unraveled/-/tree/master) provides the public APT-data basis, which fits the user's public-data requirement. Model/configuration/split receipts are retained locally. A seven-seed addendum supports the original-family effect but is not external-domain replication; its protected DE score is variable, and shuffled stream order reduces the gain. A DAPT2020 test already showed negative transfer.

The decisive later [UNSW-NB15 preregistered adjudication](C:/Users/garyp/OneDrive/Documents/codex/reports/tta_streaming_apt/px001_r2_unsw_independent_20260731/aws_full_retry1/px001_r2_unsw_independent_20260731/preregistered_adjudication.json) is **NOT REPLICATED UNDER PREREGISTERED UNSW-NB15 PROTOCOL**, passing **0/6 criteria**. Mean macro-F1 delta is **−0.017344**, target F1 delta **−0.029922**, protected F1 delta **−0.027973**, and override rate **7.053%**. **0/5 seeds** improve macro-F1; the canonical macro bootstrap lower bound is **−0.027371**. Older “lead positive” language must not override this result.

The completed paper can defend a narrower contribution: a large source-family improvement with measured external failure, showing where selective adaptation is unreliable. A plausible new RQ is whether a development-calibrated applicability/rollback rule can reject harmful adaptation on unseen shifts while protecting class utility. That requires genuinely untouched domains or shifts. Tuning such a rule on the now-known UNSW failures and presenting the same data as confirmation would not answer it.

Relative to 008, TTA has a richer real-data setting and larger original effect, but its independent replication failed. It is a credible negative-transfer/applicability thesis if the user prefers that question; it is not currently the strongest validated successful-method claim.

## Comparison with completed 008 and concrete investment choice

The current [008 results and investment assessment](C:/w/fp008/final_praxis/008_independent_evidence_audit/code_study/paper_package/RESULTS_AND_INVESTMENT.md) supports a bounded empirical paper. In **101 held-out native problems**, schema-constrained Qwen accepts **12 harmful revisions under selected disclosure versus four under uniform disclosure**: **+7.92 percentage points**, CI **[2.97, 13.86]**, exact one-sided p **0.00390625**, Holm-adjusted p **0.015625**. Useful-repair acceptance is 100/101 in both. Disjoint test roles and executable task outcomes provide a stronger outcome foundation than a style lexicon or verifier-generated label.

That finding is limited. Devstral's difference is not significant and its reused observations are not a fresh replication. The hybrid-defense H2 fails. Generated proposals provide only 34 harmful cases and show no selected-versus-uniform difference. The treatment combines filtering, omissions/fewer records, and pairing; it does not isolate one persuasion mechanism. In seven of eight additional harmful acceptances, failed tests were withheld; two of the eight pairs showed no selected records, so the result cannot uniformly be described as persuasion by reassuring passed tests. HumanEvalPack/HumanEvalPlus are public benchmarks with possible model training exposure. These limits belong in the paper, not in a footnote to a general defense claim.

**Recommended allocation:** complete and submit the narrow 008 study first, while conducting a focused PX-055 construct/novelty review using existing artifacts. If the user chooses a new primary mechanistic thesis, PX-055 is the best candidate to develop into a predeclared semantic-behavior study with independent outcomes. FalseCite is a smaller applied publication opportunity; TTA and PX-057 can support honest failure-boundary papers. None justifies further large runs merely to recover an earlier optimistic badge. The next paid experiment should answer a newly frozen question with an untouched outcome set and a clear decision threshold; this review authorizes or launches none.
