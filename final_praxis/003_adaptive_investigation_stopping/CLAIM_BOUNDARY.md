# Final Praxis 003 claim boundary

The completed discovery is **Negative** for the frozen A4 safety-gated stopping policy. Its compute and prevention metrics passed, but correctness, harm and review gates failed. No positive, near-positive or deployment-safety classification is justified.

The evidence comprises real inference on 400 generated inert authorization-triage cases, not 400 independent real incidents or an expert-labelled SOC corpus. The eight families share one explicit actor/asset/time authorization rule. Case-level intervals are conditional on this generated benchmark; they do not remove template dependence.

All policies share full eight-round traces. Savings estimate counterfactual policy-prefix token/round consumption, not savings in the scientific collection itself or in a deployed agent choosing new evidence. A1's zero early-stop harm is structural because the last observed round has no later comparison, and does not imply perfect final classification.

The frozen review design counts abstention-to-disposition changes as flips. All 400 cases triggered this rule; 40 protected cases also require terminal abstention. This is an observed and structural limitation of this policy, not proof that every alternative safety policy must fail. Numerical policies, review utility and thresholds were not changed after outcomes.

The result belongs to one model revision, seed, prompt projection, generated corpus and evidence schedule. An external incident corpus, another model, revised review semantics or human-review utility would require a new prospective experiment. Historical PX-057 outcomes supply no certified evidence here.
