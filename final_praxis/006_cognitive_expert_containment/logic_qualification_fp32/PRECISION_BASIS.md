# Prospective float32 qualification after a technical failure

The BF16 v1 run failed its registered eager-versus-SDPA next-token check before any GSM8K pilot or test generation. The reported failure was one of six fixed prompt/condition probes, in the social-ablation arm of the arithmetic probe, despite a maximum absolute logit difference of 0.15625 remaining below the v1 tolerance of 0.25. Preserve v1 as a failed numerical qualification, including its frozen protocol and outputs. These are operational facts reported by the run coordinator; the archived numerical receipt is the authority.

A separate float32 study is a reasonable engineering follow-up. PyTorch 2.6 documents finite-precision and operation-order differences, reduced-precision BF16 reductions, and the loss of mantissa precision when TF32 is enabled. It gives explicit switches to disable TF32 in matrix multiplication and cuDNN. This supports testing higher precision with TF32 disabled; it does not establish that float32 will eliminate routing or answer differences. [PyTorch 2.6 numerical accuracy](https://docs.pytorch.org/docs/2.6/notes/numerical_accuracy.html)

PyTorch also states that SDPA outputs can differ between backend kernels. Its math implementation retains float intermediates for BF16 inputs, while implementation choices still affect results. Therefore small logit differences, exact next-token agreement, and routing diagnostics provide distinct evidence. Agreement on a few probes cannot establish universal backend equivalence. [PyTorch 2.6 SDPA](https://docs.pytorch.org/docs/2.6/generated/torch.nn.functional.scaled_dot_product_attention.html)

Freeze the new study before execution in a separate folder/run: float32 parameters and computation, both TF32 switches false, maximum absolute cache/full and eager/SDPA logit difference 0.001, and identical argmax on all six probes. The 0.001 tolerance is a prospective engineering acceptance threshold, not a value justified by a task-accuracy theorem or reported by MiCRo. A second failure stops this protocol; do not relax the threshold after seeing it.

Keep the same untouched 16 training-pilot and 256 test identities, prompt, decoding cap, three arms, exact scoring, continuation gates, and runtime/cost limits. Both precision studies and their outcomes must remain visible. Passing the float32 checks permits the registered useful-logic qualification only; it does not rehabilitate BF16 v1 or establish a new containment method. Float32 increases memory and potentially runtime, so resource failure remains a legitimate incomplete outcome.
