# Candidate 010: independent challenge to the next design

Internal research note, September 14, 2026. This is a bounded primary-source review of the **prospective** method: fixed commissioning reference, adaptive operational context, and constrained admission of suspicious channels. It does not amend Q1, assess its running GPU outcomes, certify novelty, or launch an experiment. Q1 forbids HAI test scoring; this review did not inspect HAI held-out outcomes. The proposed full method and its held-out efficacy remain untested.

**Recommendation:** conditionally invest in one small, newly frozen development design if Q1 passes all its stated execution and chronology gates. Do not promote a loading/reproduction pass into a new-defense result. The strongest remaining contribution would be an explicitly scoped improvement in persistent-attack retention **and** legitimate recovery under observable trust assumptions, with measured cross-channel contamination. A generic reference-plus-adaptation method is already crowded.

## Two issues to resolve before a method freeze

**1. Observable information must justify the trust partition.** If an attack and a legitimate change produce identical observed histories and identical available metadata, any deterministic detector returns the same decision; randomized detectors have the same decision distribution. The Q1 relabeling control correctly exposes this. Recovery based only on persistence can admit a persistent attack; permanent refusal to adapt can turn a legitimate change into permanent alarms. A signed controller-mode change, separately protected sensor, or predeclared physical constraint could provide distinguishing information, but its authenticity is an assumption that must be tested when violated. Dataset attack labels cannot supply this information online.

**2. Admission mass is not forecast influence.** The proposed constraint `sum(untrusted weights) <= rho` limits coefficients. With unrestricted values, a single input can have arbitrarily large weighted magnitude even at small positive weight. Moreover, a nonlinear forecaster, its normalization, channel interactions and later updates can amplify a small input change. For a fixed linear mixture, bounding each admitted replacement displacement by `D` gives a one-step mixture displacement bound `rho*D`. Extending that to a forecaster requires an appropriate sensitivity bound, fixed other inputs/weights and a declared norm. This elementary inequality is not novel, and it does not provide an attack-detection guarantee. Either enforce and verify a concrete bounded-innovation rule, or call the restriction an **admission-mass budget**, with forecast sensitivity measured empirically. Test attacks missed by the suspicious-set classifier; a cap on correctly flagged channels does not cover false negatives.

Also specify reference alignment. Reusing an old context at every clock time can merely produce a stale-phase predictor. Reference retrieval may use declared current operating mode or past information; it may not choose the most favorable commissioning window using the future target or attack label.

## Minimum comparisons that can discriminate the contribution

Use the same selected channels, commissioning/calibration data, alarm ceiling, tuning allowance and inference budget. If the proposal calls the FM twice, include a same-budget two-reference comparator and charge both calls.

1. **Current-data controls:** rolling context; the released historical implementation as a labeled diagnostic; deployable alarm-only blending; literal freeze. The historical oracle-onset comparator cannot be the sole efficacy target.
2. **Cheap dual-reference control:** fixed commissioning and rolling operational predictions with a development-fixed blend, or jointly calibrated combination of their anomaly scores. This tests whether simply keeping a second reference explains the gain. A naive OR of alarms must be recalibrated to the same false-alarm ceiling.
3. **Robust update control:** innovation clipping/Huber-style updates and a simple change detector using the same residuals, with the same reset/recovery opportunity. Compare fixed versus adaptive calibration separately. This tests whether the new policy is only clipping or threshold adaptation in different terminology.
4. **Prior drift/anomaly family:** AD&DD or VAE4AS, with source availability and deviations disclosed. Their model training cost differs; retain a same-forecaster simplified dual-window control to isolate process effects. Do not claim a state-of-the-art comparison without qualifying the selected implementation.
5. **Cross-channel ablations:** full method minus immutable reference; minus channel admission; minus cross-channel restriction; minus recovery. Add matched per-channel/univariate forecasting and a simple multivariate linear or PCA residual baseline. Otherwise a gain could come from avoiding harmful multivariate mixing rather than the proposed restriction.

## Specific falsification tests for the next development protocol

| Test, fixed before outcomes | What would weaken or falsify the intended contribution |
|---|---|
| Benign mode change and malicious replay/step with matched duration and amplitude; include the identical-observation relabeling control. | A claim to infer intent from indistinguishable observations, or retention achieved only by refusing all legitimate adaptation. |
| Slow ramps below the alarm threshold; intermittent attacks; contamination of the commissioning interval; attacks during recovery. | Protection works only for abrupt, already-detected attacks or assumes a perfect initial reference without disclosure. |
| Perturb one channel while holding the others fixed; repeat with correlated multi-channel perturbations and a declared trusted-channel compromise. | No measurable cross-channel effect exists to control, or the claimed protection disappears under the admitted attack set. Record per-channel forecast/score changes as well as episode alarms. |
| Sweep amplitude while holding admission mass fixed; test scale/normalization changes, and cases misclassified as trusted. | Large forecast changes despite a claimed influence bound, or evasion by moving outside the flagged set. |
| Reference staleness across normal regimes; shuffled graph/provenance placebo; equal-budget plain dual-reference policy. | Gains disappear against the simpler same-budget policy, depend only on a favorable reference phase, or are unaffected by the supposedly causal graph/provenance mechanism. |
| Predeclared local, offline black-box query budget against the detector, compared with zero-query replay; no live infrastructure. | Only nonadaptive attacks are detected, while the paper is described as covering adaptive adversaries. This is a future-work extension, not an automatic novelty claim. |

## Public-data and investment boundaries

The [HAI repository](https://github.com/icsdataset/hai) supplies public industrial-testbed telemetry and attacks. Its normal/attack labels do not themselves establish independently authenticated commands, trusted hardware channels, or annotated benign regime transitions. Verify those fields and assumptions before assigning them such roles. If benign interventions or provenance are simulated, label that evaluation synthetic or semisynthetic. Keep natural HAI episodes as a separate outcome cohort; modified versions of one episode are paired variants, not independent incident replications. The pinned source audit preserves the repository's inconsistent license metadata and avoids redistributing raw data.

Freeze complete-run/episode splits, normal-data thresholds, scaling, graph fitting, reference retrieval and recovery rules before opening held-out outcomes. Report initial delay, late-episode retention, normal false alarms, recovery delay and resources. Define whether an alarm means a pointwise flag or a deduplicated incident notification; otherwise an apparent retention improvement can be purchased by repeated alerts. Bootstrap at independent run/episode units, not overlapping windows. The suggested +5 percentage-point benefit and +1 false alarm/day margin are provisional application choices, not guarantees derived from literature.

If Q1 fails its declared accounting, chronology, model-feasibility or required base-reproduction gates, hold the new efficacy study until the failure is resolved under a separate recorded change. If it passes, first test whether the full policy beats the deployable freeze/blend, simple dual-reference and robust-update controls on development data while meeting a fixed recovery budget. **Stop scaling if it only beats rolling context, needs attack labels to recover, or has no advantage after matching compute and alarm rate.** A careful negative or tradeoff result may still support a bounded study, but the current qualification cannot establish publication readiness.
