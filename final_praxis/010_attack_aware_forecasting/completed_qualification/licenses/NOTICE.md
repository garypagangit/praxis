# Data attribution and redistribution notes

The calibration artifacts derive from the public TSB-AD-U example `001_NAB_id_1_Facility_tr_1007_1st_2014.csv` (SHA256 `e3e67660bbaa840ef24e70b38f0b384d41c7e2bce71bf0edbab65565a40a7584`). TSB-AD identifies it as NAB data. Retained `forecasts.npz` includes `y_true`, overlapping windows of the example's source observations, with missing targets padded by NaN. Per-position calibration records also carry source anomaly labels. These are derived benchmark data, alongside our model predictions and scoring outputs; they are not solely generated model output. This conversion changes the representation and repeats observations across forecast horizons, without claiming ownership of the source data.

Numenta's pinned repository revision `ea702d75cc2258d9d7dd35ca8e5e2539d71f3140` changed its license to MIT on 3 December 2024. We preserve the full Numenta copyright and MIT permission notice in [NAB_LICENSE_MIT.txt](NAB_LICENSE_MIT.txt). See the [original license](https://github.com/numenta/NAB/blob/ea702d75cc2258d9d7dd35ca8e5e2539d71f3140/LICENSE.txt) and [license amendment commit](https://github.com/numenta/NAB/commit/ea702d75cc2258d9d7dd35ca8e5e2539d71f3140).

TSB-AD licenses its preprocessing and curation under Apache-2.0, while instructing users to retain underlying dataset terms. Its [dataset table](https://thedatumorg.github.io/TSB-AD/) still labels NAB ?GPL?; this differs from the later license amendment in the original NAB repository. We record that discrepancy instead of silently treating the curation license as the data license. The full curation license is preserved in [TSBAD_LICENSE_APACHE_2_0.txt](TSBAD_LICENSE_APACHE_2_0.txt), copied from [the exact TSB-AD tree](https://github.com/TheDatumOrg/TSB-AD/blob/6beac72e11d1155ade40870492c00d0d1cfdcaaf/LICENSE). The source URLs, timestamps, bytes, Git blob checks and hashes are in [LICENSE_PROVENANCE.json](LICENSE_PROVENANCE.json).

Please cite:

- Liu, Qinghua, and John Paparrizos (2024). *The Elephant in the Room: Towards A Reliable Time-Series Anomaly Detection Benchmark*. NeurIPS 2024 Datasets and Benchmarks. [Official project](https://thedatumorg.github.io/TSB-AD/).
- Ahmad, Subutai, Alexander Lavin, Scott Purdy, and Zuha Agha (2017). *Unsupervised real-time anomaly detection for streaming data*. Neurocomputing. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.neucom.2017.04.070).

The full third-party source repositories, pretrained weights and HAI raw files remain in external caches. No HAI sensor or attack-label values are redistributed here. The completed synthetic records were generated by our declared simulations.
