# Final Praxis 001 claim boundary

The experiment can establish measured acceptance, rejection, and disagreement rates for one frozen Qwen agent, one frozen Mistral judge, 20 inert JSON task templates, controlled state interventions, and a transcript-only observation regime.

It cannot establish natural error prevalence, malicious model intent, safety of production agents, superiority over all judges, universal completeness of machine-checkable postconditions, or broad novelty. Deterministic accuracy on a corpus labeled by the same semantic predicates is structural. A positive result must therefore be described as a measured judge gap under bounded observation conditions.

The complete-state diagnostic tests whether hidden collateral information explains judge errors. If errors disappear when the judge sees authoritative state, discussion must identify information availability as the explanation supported by that diagnostic, rather than crediting an intrinsically superior evaluator architecture.

The 400 units are repeated parameterizations of only 20 templates; IDs do not create 400 independent kinds of task. Single-goal templates collapse some failure categories and lack distinct multi-operation alternate orders. Natural execution state is preserved and reported separately from controlled stress outcomes. No replication or deployment result is implied.
