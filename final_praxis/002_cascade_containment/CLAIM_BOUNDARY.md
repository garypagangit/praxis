# Final Praxis 002 claim boundary

The experiment evaluates controlled upstream field corruption across three real model roles in 60 paired toy scenarios. It can test containment, clean task utility and downstream error persistence under four fixed gate placements. It does not measure the natural frequency of model errors or establish general SOC security.

All agents receive authoritative evidence and can repair errors. No tool executes outside the symbolic local action registry. Deterministic final-action checking guarantees action validity within that registry by construction; this guarantee alone is not a scientific contribution. Any positive classification additionally requires the frozen phenomenon, utility, propagation and handoff-placement gates.

Three roles of one Qwen model are not three independently trained systems. Results do not generalize automatically to other models, heterogeneous teams, realistic incident corpora, adaptive adversaries or hidden evidence. Controlled origin is known for the injected field; attribution of additional natural downstream errors remains limited. Generic cascade analysis and deterministic boundaries already have substantial prior work.

No discovery or replication outcome is claimed in this pre-result document. After independent verification, the final determination must state every failed and passed gate without importing historical Praxis results.
