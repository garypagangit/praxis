# Final Praxis 002 — source-grounded novelty review

Review date: 2026-09-08. Decision: **NOVELTY_GATE_PASS for a narrow comparative experiment; architecture and generic cascade-tracking novelty excluded.**

This supplements the earlier gate with primary-source searches covering 2024–2026 agent-security benchmarks, deterministic defenses and multi-agent failure propagation. It is a scoped review, not proof that no directly comparable paper exists. The 2026 propagation/attribution work narrows the claim beyond the earlier generic stage-attribution sentence.

| Primary source | Year/status | Task and intervention | Truth/metrics in the source | Overlap and the remaining scoped comparison |

Our small symbolic corpus is not a stronger realism benchmark; it isolates four handoff/final-action gate placements. |

Our evaluation does not claim a new diagnosis algorithm; it measures output persistence and containment under frozen gates. |

**Surviving sentence:** prior work already measures agent security, deterministic policy enforcement, multi-agent error propagation and failure attribution; Final Praxis 002 tests a narrowly specified, paired comparison of handoff-only, final-action-only, combined and ungated boundaries under controlled upstream field corruption, with machine-checkable final action truth and a frozen clean-utility requirement.

The distinction is a bounded empirical comparison, not an assertion that every component or metric is new. If the ungated model repairs the perturbations, the phenomenon gate fails. If clean utility fails, safety cannot be promoted. If final-action-only behavior makes handoff-specific benefit unsupported, that claim fails. Broader publication novelty remains a review judgment; the reproducible experiment can still yield a defensible negative result.
